## Rule families (R# = reason or classification defect, D# = derivation defect, M# = mapping)
- **R1. `financial_institution_interest_income_not_revenue` is applied to non-financial companies.** Seen at NUE (steel, op income + opex) and D (utility, cost_of_revenue + gross_profit + opex). Population count (2026-10-20 coverage): 328 companies carry that reason, **186 have SIC 6000-6799, 142 do not**. The trigger appears to be that the company also files `InterestAndDividendIncomeOperating` (it is in NUE's mapped revenue tags). The cell is blank for a different, true reason (no operating-income or gross-profit subtotal), so the user sees a false explanation. Fix: gate the reason on sector, otherwise use "no subtotal presented". Reach: 142 companies, 3 of 10 in this shard.
- **D1. Per-share metrics pair equity and shares on identical `period_end`.** `book_value_per_share` is blank at 8 of 10 (`incomplete:numerator`; only NUE and TFC are filled). For DVN only 1 of 127 stored periods has a value, NXPI 7 of 69, AME 16 of 123. The share-count series uses cover-page dates (for example 2026-07-28), while equity exists at balance-sheet dates (2026-06-30), so the two never meet; the same class feeds net_cash_per_share, fcf_per_share and piotroski's `missing:shares_outstanding(2025)`. Equity is present at every one of the 10 and matches the filing. FILING-CHECKED for the equity side. Fix: pair each balance-sheet date with the nearest share count at or before the cover date (or use the statement-of-equity share count). Reach: very large (per-share metrics at most companies).
- **M1. Oil-and-gas capex.** `PaymentsToExploreAndDevelopOilAndGasProperties` (FANG capex) and `PaymentsToAcquireOilAndGasProperty` are unmapped, so FANG loses 12 FCF-family cells. Reach: E&P names; needs a coexistence check (the first can be a component, the second an acquisition, per doc 03 combined-line rule: do not map acquisitions as capex).
- **W3-family. Operating income derived as revenue minus `OperatingExpenses`.** The derivation is valid only where `OperatingExpenses` excludes income tax and cost of revenue. At DVN the filed `OperatingExpenses` equals total expenses plus income tax. Population: **168 companies** use exactly that two-input derivation for `operating_income_resolved` (103 with a 2026 period). Each needs a check that `OperatingExpenses` is the statement's own operating-cost subtotal. Reach: 168 companies; DVN confirmed wrong.
- **W1-family. 52/53-week fiscal years get the wrong fiscal_year.** NUE labels 2026 as 2027. Companies whose year ends within days of Dec 31 (NUE and peers) will have the same problem every 53-week cycle. Check by comparing `core.period.fiscal_year` to SEC companyfacts `fy` for the latest filing.
- **0-facts family.** NXPI and D (and SPGI and CB from earlier shards) have a latest 10-Q with no facts, while SEC's own companyfacts also lacks them even though the filing has XBRL. This is why the page stops a quarter short. A population count was attempted and timed out at 50 s; it needs a chunked run. The fix path in PLAYBOOK is to parse the filing's own XBRL instance.
- **Non-dividend-payer reasons.** KEYS and NTRA show 8-10 cells each with `NO REASON` or `missing:...` where the true cause is "company pays no dividend". Same for buybacks. A reason of `no_dividend_history` exists for one cell only (`dividend_growth_streak`).
- **YTD-only per-share dividend tag.** AME's DPS is filed as a six-month figure only, so dividend_yield is blank for a dividend payer. NUE uses a `...Declared` tag and is blank for a different reason (W1).
