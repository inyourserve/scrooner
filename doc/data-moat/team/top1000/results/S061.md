## Rule families
- **M1. Revenue by tag-name priority picks a minor revenue sub-tag (NEW for insurers).** CNA revenue = `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax` (367M for a 3.8B-revenue quarter) while `Revenues` is also filed. Insurers and diversified financials often tag only a small ASC 606 part under the 606 tag and the total under `Revenues` or an extension. Reach (cheap query not run): needs a check of companies where `Revenues` exceeds the chosen revenue by over 3x. Mechanism: revenue chosen by tag-name priority, not by statement structure. Impact on this shard: 1 of 10 wrong.
- **M2. `institutional_ownership_pct` implausible.** Blank with `implausible:institutional_shares_exceed_shares_outstanding` at BJ, FN, MANH, IT (4 of 10). Suspect the numerator is a 13F sum over a quarter whose reported shares are not netted for duplicates, or the denominator is a stale/low share count (cover count after buybacks). Mechanism: ratio of a 13F quarter-end holding sum to a cover-page share count of a different date; no de-duplication of share classes or options. Check family: PLAYBOOK ownership entries (NEW if not already listed).
- **D1. Per-share metrics pair balance-sheet-date numerators with cover-date share counts** (known). `book_value_per_share` `incomplete:numerator` at 7 of 10 (BJ, BMNR, IT, GL, MANH, CG, DKS denominator); `net_cash_per_share`, `piotroski_f_score` (`missing:shares_outstanding(2026)/(2025)` at DKS, IT) follow.
- **D1b. Outstanding vs issued (NEW).** GL's filer tags outstanding equal to issued (92,218,183 while 15.16M shares sit in treasury); our balance-sheet-date value is therefore 17% high. Cover value is right. Mechanism: filer-tagged balance-sheet share count is not cross-checked against issued minus treasury or the cover count.
- **R1. `financial_institution_interest_income_not_revenue` wording is wrong for insurers and asset managers** (known R1 from S023; here applied to GL, CNA, CG). Right sector bucket, wrong label: insurers and asset managers have no operating-income/gross-profit subtotal because of their statement layout, not because interest income is not revenue. - **R2. Empty-for-everyone cells carry no reason.** `bdc_total_investment_income`, `collaborative_arrangement_revenue`, `net_lease_revenue` are unexplained at all 10 companies (30 of the 190 unexplained cells here).
