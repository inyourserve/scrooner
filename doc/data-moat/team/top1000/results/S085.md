- **F3. Multi-class cover page shares (NEW).** TAP and NTSK have no usable recent `dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding` (Class A/B cover facts are dimensional); TAP even keeps old zero-valued rows that count as "has value". Mechanism: the cover-page parser drops dimensional facts, then the cell reads as filled or blank depending on the stale remnant. Consequences: book value per share, net cash per share, piotroski, dilution metrics.
