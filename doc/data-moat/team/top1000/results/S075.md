- **NEW N1. Revenue resolved from a sub-line tag.** FCFS: `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax` is only retail merchandise sales; face total is `Revenues`. Mechanism: tag-name priority picks the ASC 606 tag; a filer that uses it for one component of a multi-line revenue block gets a partial revenue for every period. Population not counted. A check: where `Revenues` fact exists and exceeds the resolved revenue by more than ~10 percent in the same period.
