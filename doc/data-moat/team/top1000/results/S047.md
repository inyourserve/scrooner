## Rule families (R# reason defect, D# derivation, M# mapping, W# wrong value)
- **W-REV. Revenue sourced from the partial `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax` slice when the company also files `us-gaap:Revenues` as the statement total (NEW).** Seen at BG (4,660 vs 21,861 M) and SBAC (51 vs 715 M). Population (live count over `company_concept_lineage`, revenue rows with a value): **1,556 of 4,562 companies have both tags in `mapped_tags_filed` with the partial tag chosen**; the other 281 have `Revenues` chosen. Most of the 1,556 are probably the same value under both tags (no harm); the exposed ones are those where the two differ. A cheap detection rule: stored revenue below cost_of_revenue, or operating income above revenue. Needs a chunked comparison of latest-period values to size the real error set. Fix: prefer the statement total (tag priority or a check that the chosen tag's value is within tolerance of the other).
- **0-facts / SEC-lag (known, PLAYBOOK B1).** BG's Q2 10-Q has 0 facts, and SEC companyfacts holds no fact filed after 2026-07-01 for Bunge even though the R-pages exist; the cause is upstream of the pipeline. Fix path: parse the filing's own XBRL instance.
