## Rule families
- **REV-TAG-PRIORITY (NEW; related to DVN in S023).** When a filer reports both `Revenues` (statement total) and `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax` (contract subset) and they differ, Mapper stores the contract tag. LNG understates Q2 by 55 and overstates 6M by 663; MPLX understates by 697 (21%). Fix: choose the tag that equals the statement's total-revenue line when both exist (prefer `Revenues` when it is larger and the contract tag is a component), with a coexistence check. Population: not measured (a core.fact join across the population timed out at 50 s); needs a chunked run. Reach is likely every company with lease, equity-method, derivative or financing revenue outside ASC 606 (pipelines, REITs, energy, truck makers with finance arms).
- **W3-family (existing, from S023).** Confirmed again at AFL: `Revenues - OperatingExpenses` for an insurer yields 70.9% operating margin. The S023 count of 168 companies includes this pattern; AFL needs a sector rule (insurers present no operating income: show NR-F, do not derive).
- **PERIOD-LABEL-DUPLICATE (NEW or generalization of NUE W1).** For non-calendar and late-year-end filers, Scrooner labels the third and fourth quarters of older fiscal years with Q1 and Q2 (TEL: FY2025 "Q1" appears twice, once for Dec 2024 and once for Jun 2025). Population (cheap query, end_date from 2024-01-01, quarterly-length periods, same company, fiscal_year and fiscal_period, more than one distinct end date): **224 companies, 322 duplicate label groups.** Current-year periods are labelled correctly, so the damage shows as `missing:prior_period` on YoY/CAGR, TTM chains and Piotroski. DATA-INFERRED but unambiguous.
