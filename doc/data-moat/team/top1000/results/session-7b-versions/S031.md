## Rule families
- **NEW. Revenue resolves to the ASC 606 subset tag when the statement total is a different tag (HIG, IBKR).** `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax` is picked first; `Revenues` (the statement total, 7,263M at HIG, 1,896M at IBKR) is also filed and larger by 2.5x to 65x. Impact: revenue, margins, P/S, EV/Sales, growth rates, `operating_income_resolved` derived from revenue minus `OperatingExpenses`. Typical at insurers, brokers, asset managers whose "Revenues" includes investment and interest income. Population not counted (needs a chunked `core.fact` query); both brief checks (insurers, brokers) hit it, ARES's stored revenue matches the statement. Fix rule: compare candidate revenue tags in the same period and use the larger when the smaller is under (say) 80% of it and the larger is presented on the statement face.
- **NEW. Per-share EPS facts missing from `core.fact` at dual-class or per-class-EPS filers (WEC and ARES; IBKR is fine).** Statement shows EPS but the 10-Q has none stored; lineage shows an older period as "latest value", so coverage reports no gap while the page shows a stale or empty EPS. Cause in the XBRL (dimension on the EPS fact, or non-standard tag) was not verified. Hand to tag expert/engineering: take EPS filings where `EarningsPerShareDiluted` is present in the filing's R-page but absent from `core.fact`.
- **NEW. Zero is treated as a gap (UI debt).** A balance-sheet line shown as 0 (or only present as a prior-period comparative) becomes `missing:total_debt(2026)`. The correct treatment is total_debt 0 and zero_debt true when no debt line exists in the current balance sheet but is present in the prior period. Population not counted; check companies with `zero_debt` blank and `Debt` facts only in prior periods.
- **NEW. Class-A-only market cap (IBKR; probable at other Up-C structures such as ARES, and any Class A/B or LLC-unit issuer).** Cover share count covers Class A only while 77% of IBKR's earnings belong to noncontrolling interests. The ranking (#302) uses a figure that is about a quarter of the company's value. Needs a ruling: either show "Class A market cap" explicitly, or add noncontrolling unit counts.
- **Existing R1 reason defect (S023): `financial_institution_interest_income_not_revenue` on non-financials.** RBLX (software, gross_profit and operating_expenses). HIG and IBKR carry it correctly (financial).
- **Existing D1: per-share pairing.** `book_value_per_share` blank at 7 of 10 (PEG, UI, KVUE, TWLO, IBKR, HIG, ARES), `incomplete:numerator/denominator`.
- **Capex mapping (new tags for the family):** `PaymentsToDevelopSoftware` plus `PaymentsToAcquireOtherProductiveAssets` (TWLO), net `PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment` as net disposals (HIG), IBKR unidentified. Combined-line and net-line rules apply, needs tag-expert ruling.
- **Non-payer reasons (existing).** TWLO, RBLX, MTD, IBKR-buybacks: about 10 dividend cells each show NO REASON or `missing:` instead of "does not pay a dividend". Only one cell per company has `no_dividend_history`.
