## Rule families
- **NEW F-1. A company-level "revenue" that is a sub-line of a bank/fintech total (SOFI).** Revenue 12% of the filed "Total net revenue". Affects any bank-like filer that presents "Total net revenue" (interest income net plus noninterest income) with company-specific extension tags. Population not counted (needs a chunked `canonical_fact` revenue vs filed comparison). Fix: for banks use net interest income + noninterest income, never a single sub-component.
- **NEW F-2. Filer-tagged "shares outstanding" that is really shares issued (IP, 627M issued vs 529.6M outstanding).** Balance-sheet-date share counts then disagree with cover counts by treasury stock. Population unknown; detectable by comparing the balance-sheet value to the next cover value for the same company (a drop greater than 10% with no event).
- **NEW F-3. Net income stored consolidated (including NCI) instead of attributable (CF).** Inconsistent with EPS and parent equity. Population: companies with NCI above about 5% of net income; not counted. Fix rule: net_income = attributable to parent when both are filed.
- **NEW F-4. Partnership/unit-based equity and per-unit EPS tags not mapped (WES).** `Total partners' capital`, `Net income per common unit - basic`, distributions per unit. Blocks price_to_book, ROE, ROIC, basic EPS, DPS for MLPs and LPs. Population: all LPs/MLPs in the universe; count not run.
- **NEW F-5. Quarterly capex derived by YTD subtraction goes negative when the filed line is net of disposals (LEN).** A guard (reject derived negative capex and fall back to YTD or leave blank) is needed.
- **Known: 0-facts family (PLAYBOOK B1).** WES Q2 10-Q (`0001423902-26-000093`) has 0 facts. Same as NXPI and D in S023.
- **Known: wrong fiscal_year label (S023 W1 family).** NTNX cover share count dated after year-end labeled FY2027.
