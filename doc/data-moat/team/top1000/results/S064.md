## Rule families (tag: PLAYBOOK name or NEW)
- **NEW-A. Multi-class cover shares leave shares_outstanding blank.** ASTS (3 classes), WTS (2 classes): `EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding` is filed per class member, so the plain fact never exists. About 12 downstream cells blank per company, and market cap silently uses weighted-average diluted shares (ASTS W2, WTS 0.3%). Population count not run (needs a company-chunked query). Mechanism: dimensional cover facts dropped before mapping.
- **NEW-B. EPS filed only inside a class member.** ASTS EPS is stale at 2022 while lineage reports `has_value=true`; coverage therefore counts a four-year-old EPS as filled. Mechanism: has_value looks at any period, not the latest, in lineage; combined with dimensional EPS. Same shape as NEW-A.
- **NEW-C. net_income source tag `ProfitLoss` wins over `NetIncomeLoss`.** AR stores income including noncontrolling interests while EPS is attributable. Population unknown; test: companies whose net_income lineage `source_tags` is `ProfitLoss`.
- **M1 (known, E&P capex).** AR confirms the FANG finding from S023 with real lines: drilling and completion costs, unproved properties additions, other PP&E additions; the one mapped tag stopped in 2023. Cascade of 10 to 12 FCF-family cells.
- **NEW-D. Quarter-end balance tag loses to cash-flow tag for cash.** BWA cash is stale at 2025-12-31 although `CashAndCashEquivalentsAtCarryingValue` is mapped and filed for 2026-06-30. Mechanism: priority picks the restricted-cash-inclusive cash-flow tag when both exist, and it is not filed for quarter-end balance-sheet dates in the 10-Q.

- **Non-dividend-payer reasons (known).** BTSG, IONQ, ASTS, DVA, CORT, AR each show 6 to 10 dividend cells with NO REASON or a `missing:` text; CORT shows nonsense `prior_period(2010_FY)`. cash_returned_to_shareholders `incomplete:add` at DVA, BTSG, IONQ, CORT, CR, WTS, ASTS because a missing dividend is not treated as zero.
