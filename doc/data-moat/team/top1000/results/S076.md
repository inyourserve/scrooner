## Rule families
- **Bank revenue = ASC 606 subset (KNOWN, fix-register #1; ZION is a new filing-checked example).** `revenue` for a bank resolves to `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax` (fee income only), giving a 321% net margin and a 17x P/S. Mechanism: tag-name priority with no bank revenue definition. Population: a cheap count was not run (it needs a chunked scan); the bank cohort in fix-register #1 is the reach.
- **0-facts latest 10-Q (KNOWN, PLAYBOOK B1): FRT and VICR.** Both filings have XBRL (FilingSummary 42 reports for FRT; R-pages read for both) but SEC companyfacts carries 0 facts for those accessions, so our table has none. Both companies are one quarter stale on every TTM metric. Population count not run (50 s timeouts in earlier shards).
- **NEW: restated or discontinued-operations comparatives overwrite a single quarter (APTV).** After a spin-off, the latest 10-Q's restated prior-year quarter replaces one old-basis quarter, so the revenue series mixes bases and TTM revenue, P/S and margins use a sum of two bases (APTV TTM 18.7B). Mechanism: "latest filing wins" per period without a basis check. Reach: any company with a divestiture or spin (APTV; others to be found by comparing Q2 comparative with the stored prior-year quarter).
- **NEW: canonical revenue takes a partial tag for the earliest year (APTV FY2010 = 29M vs 13,817M).** Same mechanism family as DVN "derivative revenue excluded", here a services-only or wrong-scale tag when the goods tag is absent that year.
- **NEW-ish: gross interest expense never stored when the filer presents only net interest (ALSN, LTH; probably VICR/CGNX NR).** interest_expense is blank with no reason and interest_coverage_ratio, net_interest_income cascade. Mechanism: concept requires `InterestExpense`; filer files `InterestIncomeExpenseNet`. Needs a reason ("presented net only") or a net-interest concept.
- **D&A filed as two lines (ALSN; same as NUE in S023).** `Depreciation` + `AmortizationOfIntangibleAssets` not combined; blanks ebitda chain (5 cells).
- **Non-payer reasons (KNOWN, S023).** APTV, LTH, VICR, AYI-like cases show missing-input codes where the true cause is "pays no dividend".
- **REIT and bank template cells with no reason (KNOWN, fix-register #1).** FRT and ZION: current_*, quick_ratio, working_capital, sga_expense, etc.
