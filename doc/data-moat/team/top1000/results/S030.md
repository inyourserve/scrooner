## Company notes
- **ILMN:** GRAIL was consolidated until the June 2024 spin-off. It was never presented as a discontinued operation: FY2023 revenue (4,504) and net income (-1,161) are the same in the FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025 10-Ks (API). So there is **no re-presentation break**. Total assets fall from 10,111 to 6,303 when GRAIL leaves, which is real. ILMN uses a 52/53-week year (FY2015 ended 2016-01-03).
- **DHI:** a homebuilder with an unclassified balance sheet, no operating-income subtotal, no interest expense line (interest is capitalized), and debt on one `NotesPayable` line of 7,112.6 (fix-register #4).
- **TTWO:** March fiscal year. Zynga closed in May 2022 and history is continuous (same CIK). FY2023-FY2025 losses come from Zynga impairments.
- **RVMD:** pre-revenue (FC R4: the operating statement starts at "Research and development"). The `pre_revenue_biotech_pharma` reason on cost of revenue and gross profit is **correct**. Collaboration revenue ran from 2018 to Q2 2023, and RVMD itself tagged 0 for Q3 2023, every 2024 quarter, FY2024 and FY2025 (API). Those zeros are reported zeros, not invented ones.
- **KR:** 52/53-week year with a **16-week Q1** (2026-02-01..2026-05-23, 111 days) and 12-week Q2-Q4. Every Q1 period in `core.period` has `fiscal_period = NULL`, so TTM fails (W17).
- **CBRE:** real-estate services. It adopted ASC 606 by full retrospective restatement, so 2016-2017 were restated gross. Warehouse lines (711) fund mortgage loans held for sale and are counted in total debt (definition question).
- **ROP:** re-presented for discontinued operations twice (2022 and 2023 10-Ks). Revenue history mixes vintages (W15).
- **EL:** Class A and Class B shares. The 10-K tags attributable net income as `NetIncomeLossAvailableToCommonStockholdersBasic`; no `NetIncomeLoss` fact appears in the 10-K for FY2021 or later.
