## Root-cause notes for the Chief
1. **Missing 2026 10-Qs (item 3) is an SEC-source gap, not our normalizer.** 4 of 4 checked accessions (plus KO) are absent from companyfacts but present as XBRL filings. Frames carries at least WELL's. This changes the fix from "debug normalization" to "add an instance or FSDS fallback for accessions that companyfacts lacks". The Data Engineer owns that fix, with a dry-run first. Population size is not measured. The detector query `core.filing` (10-K/10-Q, filed after 2026-01-01) with 0 `core.fact` rows would measure it.
2. **EPS across splits (item 1).** Even a correctly split-adjusted subtraction is wrong (1.03 vs 1.04 filed). Derived Q4 EPS should not exist; the existing "no EPS subtraction" rule needs enforcement in `normalizer/derived.py`, not a split patch.
3. **Revenue precedence for financials (item 2)** extends W1 family 2. A third variant, the ASC 606 subset, joins gross interest income.
4. **Total-vs-component arithmetic (item 5)** joins the CostsAndExpenses family: any derived operating income must check that its expense input is not a total containing its other inputs.
5. **Sector/heuristic reasons (item 6)** fire without checking the company's own facts. Three of three checked were false.
