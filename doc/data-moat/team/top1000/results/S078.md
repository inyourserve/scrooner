## Rule families
- **NEW. Multi-registrant combined filings attribute the parent's undimensioned facts to subsidiaries (EAI).** Entergy (and similar combined 10-Ks/10-Qs: other utility holding companies, bank/holding pairs) puts every co-registrant in one XBRL document with `LegalEntityAxis`. The subsidiary shows parent revenue, assets, EPS and shares; its own figures are dimensional and its 10-K is stored with 0 facts.
