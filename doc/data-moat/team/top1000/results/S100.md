## Rule families
- **W3-family (existing, PLAYBOOK / S023): operating income = Revenues minus `OperatingExpenses`.** New instances: CACC (lender) and RLI (insurer). On financial companies the derivation gives 77% and 94% "operating margin" on revenue, against pretax 29% / 37%. Population counted in S023: 168 companies use this derivation; these two are financial-sector members of it. Ask: gate the derivation off for SIC 6xxx or require the subtotal to be on the statement.
- **NEW: BDC key line not captured.** `bdc_total_investment_income` and net investment income are blank for a BDC that files `Total investment income` and `Net investment income` on R4. Population: BDCs in the full universe; BXSL has 51 unexplained blanks, the highest in the shard. Cause: the BDC data point exists but no mapping feeds it.
- **NEW: total_debt blank when debt is on the face of the balance sheet under non-standard tags.** CACC (secured financing, senior notes, revolver), WH (current portion plus long-term debt), CWST (DebtCurrent + LongTermDebtNoncurrent style lines), VRNS (convertible senior notes). Four of ten companies, each cascading to 6 to 10 cells (debt_to_equity, net_cash, roic, zero_debt, interest_coverage, debt_to_ebitda).
- **NEW: zero debt shown as missing.** DOCS has no debt lines at all; total_debt and its dependants (debt_to_equity, net_cash, roic, zero_debt) are blank instead of zero or "no debt". Wrong-zero/blank audit (fix-register #4 family).
- **Existing: non-dividend-payer / no-buyback reasons.** CACC, CWST, ECG, VRNS, DOCS all show 8 to 12 dividend/buyback cells with no reason or `missing:..._ttm`; only `dividend_growth_streak_years` has `no_dividend_history`.
- **NEW: multi-class share count leaves shares_outstanding empty.** CWST (Class A and B on cover). Cascades to 8+ per-share, dilution and ownership cells (W10). DOCS has Class A/B too but gets its count from `CommonStockSharesOutstanding`.
- **NEW: restatement basis mixed in one series (TFX).** FY2025 recast for discontinued ops is lower than the same year's original-basis 9M YTD. Quarterly standalone values cannot be built, TTM blank. Related to PLAYBOOK period/restatement families.
- **Existing: negative P/E shown as a number** (VRNS, TFX; S023 NTRA).
- **Existing: D1 (equity and shares paired on identical period_end).** book_value_per_share blank at 9 of 10 here (all except none filled in the blank lists: CACC, WH, RLI, CWST, ECG, SAIC, VRNS, BXSL, TFX show `incomplete:numerator/denominator`).
