## Rule families (R# reason defect, D# derivation, M# mapping)

Needs a coexistence check against PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment before mapping (it can be a component). Reach: not counted; cheap query `core.fact` by tag with company-count would give it.
- **M-debt-composite (NEW, related to S023 M1).** `LongTermDebtCurrent` + `LongTermDebtNoncurrent` (both filed and authoritative) do not produce `total_debt` at CDW and EVRG, while SNA (same two tags filed) has a total_debt value. Find why the composite is built for some companies only. Reach: any company whose debt is only reported as current + noncurrent without a combined line.
- **W-multiclass-shares (NEW).** Multi-class cover shares (CBRS A/B/N, TOST A/B) produce either nothing (CBRS) or a stale zero (TOST). Sum the class values of `dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding` into one point-in-time count, and never store or count a zero share count as filled. Reach: all dual/triple class IPOs (a population query is needed; the zero is the clearest single check: `shares_outstanding` value = 0).
- **W-true-zero-debt (NEW, within fix-register #4).** A company that files no debt line at all (TOST) gets total_debt blank, which cascades to debt_to_equity, roic, net_cash and piotroski. Needs "no debt tag filed and balance sheet has liabilities only from payables/accruals" to become a stored zero with provenance.
