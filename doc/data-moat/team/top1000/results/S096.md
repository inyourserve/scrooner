## Rule families
- **NEW. Revenue resolved to an ASC 606 sub-component instead of the face total** (W1, W3). At HR the correct total sits in `Revenues`, at VAL the face line is a differently-tagged "exclusive of reimbursable" figure that is not in `core.fact`. Both are wrong across 4-6 fiscal years and drive five-six downstream cells. Mechanism: tag-name priority (`RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax` wins) instead of picking the value that matches the income statement's top line. Population not counted (cheap detection: annual revenue falling more than 90% year over year; not run).
- **NEW. Per-share dividend value scaled by the statement's "$ in Millions" (MOS W6).** Population: `dividends_per_share_resolved` rows with |value| >= 1,000: **140 rows across 39 companies** (checked live). Mechanism: per-share items inherit the filing scale; also creates a -219,999.34 Q4 derivation. Downstream: `dividend_yield` 1,224,607%.
- **NEW. Capex tag `PaymentsToAcquireOtherProductiveAssets` unmapped** (MUR, HR). Companies now file their main capex line under this tag; capex stops at 2019/2023 and takes the whole FCF family (8-9 cells each) with it. Similar to S023's M1 (oil and gas capex) but a different tag; the E&P one-line "Property additions and dry hole costs" is the only capex line at MUR. Needs a coexistence check (the tag is generic).
- **NEW. Stale last-known value for a line the company no longer presents** (HRB 2014, HR 2018). `operating_income` lineage keeps a decade-old value as latest with no staleness flag, and the margin cell says `incomplete:` instead of "not presented". Mechanism: no staleness guard on latest-period selection.
- **NEW. Authoritative-flag hole** (NPO 2020-2021): all facts for two fiscal years are non-authoritative and nothing supersedes them, so canonical drops both years of revenue and net income. Population not counted.
- **R1 (known). `financial_institution_interest_income_not_revenue` on non-financials.** Seen at HRB (tax preparer), 2 cells directly and gross margin chain; HR gets the REIT variant `reit_net_interest_income_not_revenue` on an equity REIT (6 cells).

- **D1 (known). Per-share metrics pair equity and shares on different dates**: book value per share blank at all 10.
