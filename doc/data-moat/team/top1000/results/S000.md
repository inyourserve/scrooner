# S000: ranking audit (market-cap outliers)

Data Verifier/Triage, 2026-10-18. Status: **COMPLETE** (finding only. No DB writes, no pipeline edits, nothing committed.)

## Scope and method
- Population checked: the 1,300 largest active `market_cap` rows in `analytics.metric_value` (overall rank; shard rank 1000 = TFX = overall rank 1009, so this covers the list plus about 290 below it). There are 4,351 non-null and 866 null market_cap rows in total.
- For each company (read-only Supabase MCP, one query at a time, chunks of 15-40 ids, `statement_timeout` 50s), I compared the share count implied by `market_cap / price` with: the latest dei `EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding` (concept 1) and the one before it, the latest diluted WASO (935), and the latest `EntityPublicFloat` (2). Flags: mcap/float >8 or <0.05 (float dated after 2024-06), implied shares vs cover or WASO outside 0.33-3x, cover count jump >3x, cover vs WASO >3x, and nothing fresh to cross-check against.
- Two more passes: foreign-incorporated issuers (EDGAR state code letter+digit) with mcap > $1B and mcap/float >2.5 or no float (to catch ADS ratios that the 8x float check misses), and the null-market-cap list (to find large companies left out of the ranking).
- Filing checks: the cover page (R1) and income statement (R4) of the latest 10-Q/20-F on sec.gov, User-Agent `Scrooner research inyourserve@gmail.com`, 0.6s between requests (14 requests in total).
- Evidence labels: **FILING-CHECKED** means I opened the filing and the accession is cited. **DATA-INFERRED** means the finding comes from our tables only.

## 2. Root causes in code
File: `pipeline/src/scrooner_pipeline/mapper/price_metrics.py`
1. **There is no ADS or depositary ratio anywhere** (line 362, `market_cap = shares_hit[0] * price`). The price is per ADS (Alpaca ticker), but the share count is ordinary shares, both from the cover (dei ECSO) and from the balance sheet (CommonStockSharesOutstanding). Both tags map to canonical `shares_outstanding` (`analytics.concept_mapping`: CommonStockSharesOutstanding priority 1, dei ECSO priority 2, `first_match`). `_latest_instant_fact` (lines 91-107) takes whichever has the newest end date. The ratio is stated only in the cover's text (`dei:Security12bTitle`, "each representing N ordinary shares"), which we never collect. Affected: AKTX, ZLAB, SCNI, KZIA, AMRN, GPCR, DBVT.
2. **WASO fallback with no plausibility check** (`_load_latest_diluted_weighted_shares`, lines 179-205, and `_choose_shares`, lines 236-247). When the canonical cover or balance-sheet count is stale (older than 480 days, line 213) or missing (multi-class covers are dimensioned and stripped by companyfacts), the latest positive WASO is used as is. Filer scale errors (x1000) pass straight through: TBLA, RPAY. The 25% cover/WASO cross-check (lines 217, 240-243) only runs when a fresh cover count exists, so it never protects the cases that reach this fallback.
3. **WASO period ambiguity:** `order by p.end_date desc limit 1` (line 199) has no tie-break between the discrete-quarter row and the YTD row that share an end date. SPCX got the YTD 4,879M instead of the Q2 figure of 5,864M. Both understate a multi-class issuer whose true cover total is 13.18B. WASO is a period average, not a point-in-time count, so it understates after share issuance.
4. **The primary path is not checked against anything** (line 236): a fresh primary count is accepted with no comparison to float, WASO or the prior count.
5. **Population** (not in price_metrics): commodity trusts (SIC 6221) are inside `real_operating_company`.

## 3. Proposed fix (rule family MCAP-SHARES) and the guards it needs
- **R1 Cover-page total first, all classes.** Market cap shares = the sum of every class's `EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding` on the latest cover. This needs the dimensioned facts from the filing instance or FSDS: extend `core.shares_outstanding_fallback` (`company_master/shares_outstanding_fallback.py`) to sum every class row, not just one. Never pick one class as the total (domain rule 8.7). This fixes SPCX and RPAY, and probably BRK, FWONA, PLNT and UHAL. A sum is only valid when every class has the same economic claim per share. Where it does not (for example BRK-A/B convert 1:1500), it needs a conversion factor: mark those `needs_review`, do not guess.
- **R2 ADS normalization.** Collect `dei:Security12bTitle` and parse "each representing N ordinary shares". When the price ticker is the ADS, use shares = ordinary / N. Until the title is collected, apply a guard: foreign-incorporated issuer and (mcap / fresh, DQC_0222-valid EntityPublicFloat > 2.5, or no float at all with a 20-F cover) -> null with `suspect:ads_ratio` and queue for review. Do not let balance-sheet `CommonStockSharesOutstanding` win over the cover for ADS issuers.
- **R3 WASO only as a last resort, with scale guards.** Reject a WASO fact if (a) it is more than 10x or less than 0.1x the median of the company's other WASO facts in the prior 8 quarters (catches TBLA's mixed x1000 values), (b) it differs from any cover ECSO for the same company by more than 100x, at any age (DQC_0095 analogue; TBLA stale cover 336M vs 290.5B), or (c) it differs from revenue-implied plausibility (below). Prefer the discrete-quarter row over YTD on an end-date tie.
- **R4 Publish guard on the final market cap (hold for review, do not auto-correct):** any of (i) mcap/EntityPublicFloat > 8 or < 0.05, where the float is within $1M-$10T and dated within 15 months; (ii) mcap > $50B and mcap/TTM revenue > 200; (iii) cover count changed more than 3x since the previous cover, with no split ratio tag (`StockholdersEquityNoteStockSplitConversionRatio1`) in the window. CRWD and KLAC show (iii) fires on real splits, so check for the split tag. (i) also fires for controlled companies and post-float rallies, so it is a review queue, never an automatic null.
- **R5 Population:** exclude SIC 6221 commodity trusts (and other grantor-trust ETFs) from `real_operating_company`.
- **Verifier sample before release:** the 10 FILING-CHECKED rows above are the fixed regression set. After the fix, re-run this audit's checks on all 4,351 rows and open the cover of every company whose market cap moves by more than 2x.
