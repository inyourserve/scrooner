## 3. Why the other tag is not the total: five mechanisms (all FILING-CHECKED above)
1. Component: the contract tag is one revenue stream (GE, AMT, EXR, OGE, CNC, HIG, PAYX, SNA, GM).
2. Gross before deductions: the contract tag is larger than the total because contra-revenue, derivative or provision lines are negative (MA, WMB, FCX, DVN, THC).
3. A non-statement fact filed undimensioned under the total tag (filer context errors and narrative values): BLK related-party, EMR narrative, FLS intersegment 0, NTAP segment, GIS joint ventures, GE Capital quarters (ledger 2026-10-21). Any rule that reads companyfacts (dimensions stripped, so these look like totals) without knowing WHERE the fact sits in the filing will pick them.
4. Vintage or basis mix: comparing tags across different filings (AJG restated vs original; GE recast). Part of the 4,479 "differ" count is this, not a total-vs-component choice.
5. Transition-year zeros: RCFC 0.0 in pre-adoption comparative columns (OGE).
Mechanisms 1, 2 and 5 are fixed by structure; 3 is fixed only by structure (the fact is not on the statement); 4 needs a basis policy.

## 4. The general mechanism (one resolver; revenue is the first anchor)
Principle: a value is the canonical line X for a filing only when the filing itself says so: the line sits where X sits on the face statement, the filer's calculation tree puts it where X belongs, and the filing's own numbers satisfy the identity that defines X. The tag name is a hint, never the decision. This makes mechanisms 1-3 and 5 impossible by construction, because a fact that is not the face line (a note value, a segment, a related-party figure, a component) can never be selected.
Shape (per filing = per accession, per context = period + undimensioned):
1. **Locate the statement**: the income-statement role (FSDS `pre.stmt = 'IS'`, else the R-file whose title matches and whose role is in the first statements block; do not rely on FilingSummary MenuCategory: MA files its income statement as "Uncategorized").
2. **Anchor definition** (data, not code, one row per anchor): for revenue, "the last revenue-section row before the first expense/cost row, undimensioned, that is a calc child (+1) of a profit subtotal (gross profit, operating income, pretax) or the top of the revenue section". Same shape for operating income (calc parent of revenue and opex, child of pretax), net income (equals cash-flow starting line, ledger/39 identity), total debt (balance-sheet carrying lines; composition from `cal`), total equity (parent of the equity lines; L + E = A).
3. **Candidates**: every undimensioned row in that statement section, whatever its tag (standard or custom).
4. **Proofs**, all on the same accession and context: (P1) position; (P2) calc role relation (parent of the other revenue rows, child of the profit subtotal); (P3) identity: value = sum of weighted calc children, or of the presentation siblings above it in the section when the filing has no calc children (OGE), or of the face dimension members of the same tag (SNA, GM); tolerance = rounding only (each addend's decimals interval, XBRL duplicates rule, doc 23 s.1.5); (P4) label is a total/revenue label (supporting only: SNA's total is labelled "Net sales").
5. **Decision**: exactly one candidate with P1 + P3 and no contradicting P2 -> selected, record `structural_proof = {accession, role, line, calc_parent, children, identity_residual}`. A single revenue row with nothing to sum (BLK, NTAP, FLS, GIS, EMR) is selected by P1 + P2 (calc child of the profit subtotal) and is "single-line" (identity trivial; weaker, flagged). Zero candidates or two candidates that both pass -> blank with reason `revenue_total_undecidable` (sub-reasons: `no_statement_located`, `identity_failed`, `two_totals`, `total_line_not_tagged_standard` when the selected tag is custom and needs company-scoped mapping). Banks and other no-revenue-total templates are out of this anchor (data-team/32: bank revenue = NII + noninterest income, its own anchor).
6. **Agreement with the name path** (path A = today's `concept_mapping`/resolvers, path B = this): agree -> publish (certifiable); disagree -> path B's value wins only after the Verifier's zero-failure sample for that rule version; until then the cell is held as `structure_name_disagree` with both values, and each disagreement becomes a finding for the Verifier; B-only -> candidate fill, never auto-published (coexistence check + Verifier sample); A-only (structure undecidable) -> keep A only if A's tag is the one and only revenue-family tag on the face, else blank with reason. In this sample A and B disagree for 14 of 19 companies under RCFC-first and 7 of 22 displayed periods.

## 5. The revenue-total rule (instance of section 4)
Inputs per filing: income-statement presentation rows (order, tag, version, label, negating, statement, report), the filing's calc arcs (parent, child, weight, role), and the filing's undimensioned and dimensioned facts for the tags in that statement (value, decimals, period start/end, segments). Not companyfacts: it strips dimensions and custom tags, which are exactly what the identity needs (CNC `cnc:PremiumTaxRevenue`, OGE `oge:RevenuesfromAlternativeRevenuePrograms`, GE `ge:` children).
Tie-breaks, in order: (1) the candidate that is the calc parent of the other revenue candidates; (2) the candidate that satisfies the identity with the rows above it; (3) if both remaining candidates satisfy their identities, blank `two_totals` (never "largest": wrong in 5 of 19 here); (4) else blank `identity_failed`. Never a fixed tag priority.
Dimensioned and segment facts: never candidates for the consolidated total; used only as identity addends when they are the face's own breakdown (SNA, GM, GE R3 product/service). A segment total filed undimensioned (NTAP, GE Capital, GIS joint ventures) is excluded because it is not in the statement role; segment revenue stays a separate concept.
Quarters: a 10-Q is its own filing: select the 3-month total from its income statement the same way. Q4 = FY face total (10-K) minus 9M YTD face total (Q3 10-Q) only when (a) both selections are the same tag and the same identity children set, and (b) the 10-K's own prior-year column for the 9M-overlapping year shows no recast (if it does, use the 10-K's quarterly note when it has a 3-month undimensioned fact of the SELECTED tag whose four quarters sum to FY within rounding, as GE R161; a quarterly note value equal to a dimensional member in the same filing is rejected, ledger 2026-10-21 GE Capital); else blank `q4_basis_change`. 10-K quarterly notes are evidence for Q4, never for Q1-Q3 when a 10-Q exists.
Eras since 2015 (the "15 later years"): the rule is tag-agnostic, so the 2018 ASC 606 tag switch (SalesRevenueNet / SalesRevenueGoodsNet / Revenues -> RCFC), retired tags (SalesRevenueNet absent from the 2021+ taxonomy) and custom totals need no period-scoped tag list. Per company, emit an `anchor_tag_change` event when consecutive filings select different tags; check the overlap year (each 10-K repeats 2-3 years): equal value -> pure tag switch; different value -> restatement or recast, the basis label is set and the series is not spliced across bases without the basis policy (AJG, GE). Transition columns with 0.0 under the new tag (OGE 2016-17) fail the identity and are never selected. Linkbase format changes: separate `_cal.xml` (2015-2024 filings in the sample) vs linkbases embedded in the `.xsd` (BLK FY2024, NTAP FY2026); FSDS/FSN normalise both. Pre-2019 non-inline filings: R-files and calc linkbases exist (GE FY2018, EXR FY2018, OGE FY2018, AJG FY2017 opened). 2015-2016 original filings were NOT opened in this run (only their columns re-presented in 2018-2019 10-Ks): untested.

## 6. Eligible vs proposal-only
ELIGIBLE (a deterministic structural proof per filing; no perimeter decision, because the value is the filer's own printed total for the concept "total revenue as presented"): selecting the face total when P1 + P2 + P3 hold for a non-bank template, the selected tag is standard us-gaap, and the identity residual is within rounding; blanking with `revenue_total_undecidable` whenever the proof fails (a guard: only hides a value with a reason). Applies to rows 1-3, 5-20 type cases. Still subject to the strategy-40 gate and a Verifier zero-failure sample before any release.
PROPOSAL-ONLY (changes an accounting perimeter or a basis; no named accountant yet):
- Face totals that include non-revenue items: insurers' "total revenues" with realized capital gains (HIG), "total revenues and other income" (CVX; GE before 2019), equity-method income inside revenue; whether Scrooner "revenue" equals the filer's total there is a definition call.
- Gross vs net presentations: contra-revenue (MA), bad-debt provision netting pre-ASC 606 (THC), premium taxes (CNC).
- Any cross-vintage choice (restated vs as originally filed; AJG, GE recasts) and series splicing across a basis change.
- Custom-tag totals (company-scoped mapping with accession + calc path evidence).
- Q4 derivation where the 10-K and the Q3 10-Q select different tags.
- All banks, thrifts, BDCs (separate anchors).
Withdrawal criteria: any Verifier case where the selected value is not the printed total; any identity passing by sign flip; any selected fact found outside the statement role.
