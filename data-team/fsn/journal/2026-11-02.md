## What I learned
- **The SEC spec is wrong in five places that matter:**
  - DIM's key column is `dimhash`, and "no dimension" is `0x00000000`, not 0;
  - iprx starts at 0;
  - `datp` is days with sign ddate − actual, not a fraction;
  - cal `negative` is ±1, not boolean;
  - dcml −32768 marks a nil value.
- **coreg** is filled only when LegalEntity is the fact's only axis.
- **dimn=0 is not "the face".** MSFT's Product/Service revenue and cost lines are dimensional face rows, and dimn=0 includes note facts.
- **ddate can land in the month before the quarter's own month** (PepsiCo 2025-06-14 → 20250531). `sub.period` is rounded the same way.
- **January-FYE filers' `fy`** is the year their fiscal year starts.
- **Cal** is absent before 2013q3 and missing for only 1–4% of top-100 periodic filings since.
- **MSFT has nine recasts since 2014.** The biggest is ASC 606/842 in Q1 FY2018. The newest, in FY2026, moves billions between two custom CF lines while CFO stays fixed; it breaks any D&A series built on MSFT's combined line.
- **One missing FX tag** in the cash identity blanks MSFT's CFO/dividends/buybacks from FY2020. 19 top-100 names print that tag.

## What surprised me
- MSFT printed Q4 in its 10-K through FY2020. Q4 EPS is a *printed* number for those years, yet the pilot reasons it away.
- A label stays fixed while the tag changes: MSFT "Short-term debt" is `ShortTermBorrowings`, then `CommercialPaper`. Production's FY2024 debt misses 6.7B because of it.
- MSFT's FY2016 annual figures (restated ASC 606) cannot be reconciled with its FY2016 quarters (never restated).
