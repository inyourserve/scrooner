# Ranks 601-700: earlier docmaps superseded (Dhairya 2026-11-13)

The `*-docmap-2026-11-12.{md,csv}` files for ranks 601-700 committed up to ef86fd7e were written by an earlier reader
(era coverage FY2015-latest for 19 of 100 companies; its 10% check re-opened R pages by script, not by hand).
They are superseded and must not be loaded. They are being replaced, ten ranks per commit, by the shared tool
`tools/docmap.py` (de14aace) run in Actions (`.github/workflows/sec-reads-shared-tool-601.yml`) plus a hand pass in
the 401-500 format: every not_presented, combined, component and NOTE row and a seeded 10% of mapped rows
(seed '601-700:<TICKER>'), each decided on the printed R page and marked 'check: hand <R page>'.
A docmap is loadable only once its batch commit says "shared reader + hand pass".
