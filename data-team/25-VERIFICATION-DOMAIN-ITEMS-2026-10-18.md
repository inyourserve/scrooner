## C. Where doc 21 was wrong or materially incomplete (all CORRECTED rows)

1. A1: phase-in "large filers from 2009, others 2010-2011" hid the three-cohort rule ($5B-float large accelerated / other large accelerated / everyone else, by period end) and the one-year lag before notes were detail-tagged.
2. A4: "pre-XBRL history is not in structured data" is wrong. First XBRL filings tag 1-3 years of earlier comparatives, but only as re-presented later, never as an as-originally-filed vintage.
3. A6: ASC 606 "effective 2018 public" depends on fiscal year end (Apple: FY2019) and can be later for EGCs.
4. A8: ASC 842 "(2019)" depends on fiscal year end (Apple: FY2020); EGCs adopted as late as 2022.
5. A9: CECL "2020+ for large filers" is wrong on the cut-off (it is SEC filer and not SRC-eligible) and leaves out the 2023 date for SRCs and the CARES/CAA bank deferral to as late as 2022-01-01.
6. B1/B2: 52-week years are 364 days, so an exact-365 test fails them too. Only one quarter is 14 weeks, and it lengthens YTD periods.
7. B7: Item 4.02 covers interim periods. Restatements can arrive as 10-Q/A, not only 10-K/A.
8. B9: Exhibit 99.1 is convention, Item 2.02 material is furnished, and earnings-release numbers are not in XBRL.
(B3 corrects the caller's suggested example, Nike, not doc 21 text.)
