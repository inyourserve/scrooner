## 4. EPS (all 31): split adjustment and the retroactive split stage
- **All 31 proposed EPS values are exactly what the latest filing prints on its face** (FILING-CHECKED). There are 9 pure restatements (ROLR x4, EONR x2, NROM, NCRA 6M 2026 x2) and 22 that change the share basis: 13 split only (X1) and 9 split + restatement (X2).
- GURE (-3.46 -> -33.98, 6M 2024) is a reverse-split re-basing. The 10-Q/A 0001493152-26-045125 R4 shows the same net loss (37,090,050) on 1,091,562 weighted shares instead of 10,726,924 (x9.83, not exactly 10, so the share count was also revised). Q2 2024 and Q1 2024 are split only; Q1/Q2/6M 2025 also restate net loss.
- BURU: the 10-Q/A 0001193125-26-396895 R4 footnote states "Net loss per common share as restated reflects the 1-for-40 reverse stock split effected on September 1, 2026, applied retroactively. Of the $(1.42) adjustment, $(1.57) is attributable to the reverse stock split and $0.15 to the correction of the error". The 2025 periods were already adjusted for the "1-for-4.99 February 2026 Reverse Stock Split".
- NCRA: the later 10-K/A 0001683168-26-006788 R4 shows the same net loss (2,880,041) on 481,121 shares instead of 14,306,487 (x29.7).
- SVMB: the later 10-K/A 0001641172-25-008877 R4 shows Previously Reported / Adjustment / restated rows under `srt:RestatementAxis`. Net loss is unchanged; shares fall from 209,845,205 to 1,055,138 and from 1,001,276,294 to 5,315,412. That is filed as a "restatement" but is economically a share re-basing.
- **Interaction with `resolve_retroactive_split_adjustments` (code read, DATA-INFERRED for the table):**
  1. Split-adjusted resolved rows carry the split-disclosure fact id in `source_fact_ids`, so their tag set differs and the same-tag guard already excludes them. No double adjustment is possible through this function.
  2. The retroactive stage is insert-only (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`) and never re-bases an existing resolved row.
  3. `analytics.company_stock_split` has **no** split for GURE, NCRA or SVMB. For BURU it has 2024-07-23 (0.025) and 2026-02-27 (0.2) but **not** the 2026-09-01 1-for-40 split that the 10-Q/A discloses.
  So applying the 22 split-basis proposals would give these companies an EPS series that mixes post-split cells (the restated quarters) with pre-split neighbours (10x to 200x apart). That breaks growth, TTM EPS and P/E. **Verdict for the 22: as-filed correct, but NOT safe to apply cell by cell.** They belong to the corporate-actions path: register the split from the amendment's own disclosure, then re-base every period of the series. That is the Period Engineer / corporate-actions owner's call; I did not decide the method.
