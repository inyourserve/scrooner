## 1. Headline
- Of the 82 sample rows opened: **stored correct 28, proposed correct 34, both defensible (different bases) 14, neither correct 3, unverifiable 3.** 5 rows were not opened.
- **The proposed value is wrong in 31 of 82 rows** (28 stored-correct plus the 3 neither rows), and among the 37 sign flips it is wrong in 21. Even the 19 random rows checked had 6 wrong proposals.
- **Systematic cause:** the query pairs the stored `*_resolved` row with the primary concept's row purely by (company, period). It never checks that both rows come from the same XBRL tag, that the primary is filed rather than derived, or that it comes from a 10-K or 10-Q. So most proposals are not "a newer copy of the same figure". They swap in a different figure: another tag, a derived Q4, or a proxy-statement number.
- **There is one clean class:** same tag, later filing, non-derived, periodic form. That is bucket G, 1,033 proposals. Of the 25 opened there, the stored value was correct in **none**: 18 proposed correct, 6 both defensible under the latest-filed policy, 1 unverifiable.

## 4. Systematic patterns (each FILING-CHECKED on the rows cited)
1. **Derived primaries are wrong for this purpose (A).** Mixed-vintage FY-minus-9M values produce impossible results: negative revenue (DOC row 2, MUR 84), negative cost of revenue (LIFD 3), negative dividends per share (AGNT 64/65, LEA 66), negative interest expense (AIBT 11). EPS is derived by subtraction (RMNI 23/61), which doc 21 forbids. A re-presented FY minus an original 9M gives a wrong value (SPGI 15, ADC 85). **Upstream defect to route to the Period Engineer:** `core.fact` holds authoritative derived Q4 rows for EPS and DPS, and derived rows with impossible signs. The exceptions (UP 12, RTB 13/14) are cases where the *stored* Q4 was an annual figure that the filer tagged with a 3-month context; there the derived value is right.
2. **A different tag is usually a different line (D/E).** The replacement is a component or subtotal of the stored line: ANDE 74 (ASC 606 subset, 1.40B vs 11.26B), SHFH 4/5 (a cost-of-service sub-line of 0), SKYW 50 (a capex sub-line), and continuing-ops cash-flow subtotals replacing totals (XPL 53, FGNX 54, JEF 55, RDN 56, MWYN 57, AIHS 58). Or it is a segment figure (GE 82, GE Capital) or an oppositely signed filer tag (QWTR 20, CIK 2065779 row 21, BEEM 22 via `...AvailableToCommonStockholdersBasic`; ARKO 0/1 via a segment-note eliminations row). Or it is a zero from an unused tag (MOJO 26, UVSP 83). Two pairs did come out right: ProfitLoss->NetIncomeLoss (NL 70, ON 73, RIG 79) and restricted-inclusive cash->plain cash (LAD 28). Both match the concept's priority-1 definition. A higher mapping priority alone is not safe: ANDE, SHFH and MOJO were all wrong in favour of the priority-1 tag.
3. **Non-periodic sources (B).** The DEF 14A pay-versus-performance figure is rounded (AEMD 77: (13,400,000) vs (13,388,089), a 0.09% gap that passes the 0.01% rounding tolerance). 8-K recasts are re-presentations (BKD 76) and need an explicit policy (doc 21 §8 rule 2: filter by form).
4. **Arithmetic stored rows (C) should be replaced.** Revenue minus opex left out cost of revenue or other operating lines (VIAV 8, PAR 9, PANW 10), and the filed OperatingIncomeLoss is correct.
5. **Same-tag later filings (G) are mostly genuine refreshes.** These are 10-Q/A and 10-K/A restatements (GAMG, GURE, GFUZ, BURU, SENEA, LGND) and later re-presentations (CMTL, LEEN, WRLD, JEF, FCX, SPGI Q3). The proposals follow the latest-filed policy (decisions.md 2026-10-18).
6. **Filer period-label errors are common and invisible to the dates.** Examples: LGND 10-K/A 0000886163-14-000119 has column headers shifted one year; UP, RTB and AIBT tagged annual values with a 3-month context; HALO's FY2016 quarterly table holds 2015 values; ESMC has a 2026-06-30 balance in a 10-Q filed 2025-11; CSUI mis-tagged total assets. Here the stored value was the corrupted one, and the proposal fixes it only by coincidence.

## 5. What `authoritative_older_than_stored` does and does not protect
It compares `max(filing_date)` over the stored row's source facts with `max(filing_date)` over the primary row's source facts, and skips the update only when the primary's date is **strictly earlier**. It skipped 6,431 revenue candidates alone.
- **Protects:** a newer restated or re-presented value (the latest-filed figure, per policy) from being overwritten by an older original-vintage authoritative value.
- **Does not protect against:** (a) a different tag or concept (subset, component, segment, continuing-ops subtotal, opposite-sign alternative tag), because tags are never compared; (b) derived primaries, whose filing date is the newer 10-K, so mixed-vintage FY-minus-9M values pass; (c) equal dates (2,794 proposals have the same filing date on both sides, and the guard passes all of them, so it cannot adjudicate a same-filing disagreement); (d) non-periodic forms such as 8-K recasts and DEF 14A, which are newer and pass; (e) filer period-label or context errors in either filing; (f) sign flips and zeros; (g) differences between rounding precision and the 0.01% tolerance (AEMD); (h) multiple filings on one day (dates have no time or acceptance ordering). It can also block a correct update when the *newer* filing is the corrupted one. That was not seen in this sample, but it is not excluded.
