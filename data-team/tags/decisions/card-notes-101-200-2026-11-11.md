# Tag card notes: cohort ranks 101-200 (Ritwik cloud, 2026-11-11)

For the SEC/FSN Tag Expert.
- Rules 1-19 are in `card-notes-51-100-2026-11-11.md`; these are new or extended.
- Evidence is from the FSN store (accession, line), DB reads only. None was opened on sec.gov.
- Decisions per company: `companies-101-200-exceptions-2026-11-11.csv`.

20. **IncomeLossIncludingPortionAttributableToNoncontrollingInterest is a pretax line.**
    - Its us-gaap standard label is "Income (Loss), Including Portion Attributable to Noncontrolling Interest, before Tax" (fsn.tag.tlabel).
    - It is used as the face "Income before income taxes" by MCK (0000927653-26-000069 IS line 11) and PSX (0001534701-26-000006 IS line 18).
    - Map it to income_before_tax. Together with rule 1 (IncomeLossAttributableToParent), both "...before Tax" concepts are pretax.
21. **Co-registrant filings: take only the parent registrant's statements.** One 10-K/10-Q can carry a second registrant's full statements:
    - PLD with Prologis, L.P.;
    - SPG with Simon Property Group, L.P.;
    - DUK with its six utility subsidiaries;
    - CMCSA with NBCUniversal Media LLC (to 2019);
    - URI with United Rentals (North America).

    Seen as PartnersCapital or MembersEquity "total partners' capital" / "member's equity" lines beside the parent's StockholdersEquity. Extraction must filter on `fsn.value.coreg` (empty = the parent) for every data point.
22. **Net income when the face has no "attributable to parent" line.**
    - Preferred stock exists and only "net income" (ProfitLoss) and "net income attributable to common" are printed: net_income = ProfitLoss minus the NCI line, as a face component sum.
    - Example: PNC 0000713676-26-000020 IS lines 34-35 = 6,997 - 61 = 6,936M. This is the same component rule as SCCO (Dhairya 2026-11-11).
    - "Attributable to common" is net_income only when the company has no preferred stock.
      - It is net_income at CVS, BE and (rule 10) WELL, ETN, BKNG, PH.
      - It is NOT net_income at OXY (preferred from 2019), BSX (2020-2023 convertible preferred) or PNC.
23. **Filer tag errors: map by the face label and position, and flag.**
    - CME 2025-26: "repurchase of Class A common stock" tagged PaymentsForProceedsFromDerivativeInstrumentFinancingActivities.
    - MSI 2018-12: basic and diluted EPS tags swapped.
    - LITE: cash tagged CashAndCashEquivalentsFairValueDisclosure.
    - EQIX, GLW: after-tax continuing-operations tags on the face "income before income taxes" line.
    - KMI, AON: buyback lines tagged ProceedsFromRepurchaseOfEquity (sign check).
24. **REIT capex** (doc 02 line 114) = development + tenant improvements and leasing commissions + property improvements. Example: PLD 0001193125-26-051453 CF lines 27, 30, 31.
    - Acquisitions are excluded.
    - Open: leasing commissions sit inside the TI line.
25. **Rental-fleet capex** = purchases of rental equipment + purchases of non-rental equipment and intangibles (URI, custom-namespace copies of the std tags from 2021). Proceeds are excluded.
26. **Combined-line patterns:**
    - "depreciation, amortization and impairment" (GM);
    - "depreciation, amortization and other operating gains" (CMCSA);
    - "capital expenditures and investments" (PSX, COP);
    - "capital expenditures, net (of disposals)" (SPG, TDG, CI);
    - "dividends to common and preferred" (SPG, TRGP);
    - "repurchase of shares and warrants" (KMI);
    - LP-unit plus treasury repurchases (SPG);
    - "net settlement of vested ... and repurchase" (BX).
27. **Not interest expense:**
    - insurer "interest credited to policyholder account balances" (MET);
    - a financial-services segment's "financing interest" inside cost of sales (HPE);
    - "net interest revenues" at a broker (HOOD).
28. **"Income from operations before income tax expense" at an insurer** (ALL) is the pretax line, not operating income.
29. **Pre-merger SPAC filings under the same CIK** (VRT 2018-2019, GS Acquisition Holdings) are not the company's statements. Exclude them, as a history window.
30. **MLP and LP issuers:** EPS = net income per common unit (EPD, ET, KKR to 2018). Distributions to common unitholders are labelled "distributions" (find the line; ET open).
