## Structural rules (apply to every company)

1. **IncomeLossAttributableToParent is a pretax line.** Its us-gaap standard label is "Income (Loss) Attributable to Parent, before Tax".
   - It is used as the face "income (loss) before income taxes" by MRVL (0001058057-19-000024 IS line 14), COP (2022-2024) and TMUS (2021).
   - Map it to income_before_tax, below the two standard pretax tags.
2. **The total wins over its components, except where the "total" adds non-revenue items.**
   - IBM, KLAC, VZ, GILD, DIS, BMY, FTNT, ISRG, NOW, LMT, VRTX, PFE, SCHW, WELL, TMUS: Revenues / SalesRevenueNet "total ..." wins over product and service lines.
   - COP: "Total Revenues and Other Income" (Revenues) adds equity earnings, gains and other income. Revenue is the face revenue line above it: 0001193125-20-039954 IS line 2, RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax.
3. **Revenue = the face top-line revenue as printed** (Dhairya ruling 2026-11-11).
   - MPC prints "excluding consumer excise taxes" first (IS line 2), then "including" (line 3). Take line 2.
   - ACN: total revenues including reimbursements (to FY2018).
4. **Cost of revenue = the line directly above Gross profit, as printed.**
   - LMT: "Total cost of sales" (CostOfGoodsAndServicesSold, 0001193125-16-476010 line 13).
   - LMT later: "Total operating costs and expenses" (CostOfRevenue, 0001628280-26-004195 line 13). A same-label subtotal at line 10 is not it.
5. **CostOfRevenue labelled "total costs and expenses" with no gross-profit line is not cost of revenue.**
   - BA: the line includes Boeing Capital interest expense.
   - Use "cost of products and services" (CostOfGoodsAndServicesSold), else cost of products + cost of services.
6. **No total cost line printed: cost of revenue = the sum of the lines under the cost heading.**
   - VZ: cost of services + cost of equipment.
   - TMUS: the same.
   - T (0001193125-19-045608 IS lines 6-8): equipment + broadcast, programming and operations + other cost of revenues, each excluding D&A.
   - DIS (2014-2018): cost of services + cost of products.
7. **PaymentsToAcquireProductiveAssets is not capex when a PP&E purchases line sits on the same statement.**
   - MCD 2013-2019: "purchases of restaurant businesses" (acquisitions) carries that tag beside "capital expenditures" (PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment).
8. **Equity tagged StockholdersEquityIncludingPortionAttributableToNoncontrollingInterest:**
   - With no MinorityInterest line on the same balance sheet, it is parent equity: map. Seen at COP (0001562762-21-000304 BS line 35), MO, QCOM, TJX, VRTX, CB and KLAC.
   - With an NCI line printed and no parent line: not presented. VZ 0000732712-26-000007 BS lines 42-43; T 0000732717-26-000120 lines 48-49. Total minus NCI would be a derived instant, which is never allowed.
9. **Cash line tagged CashCashEquivalentsRestrictedCashAndRestrictedCashEquivalents with no restricted-cash line on the balance sheet:** this is the face cash line. Map it.
   - GILD 0000882095-26-000006: BS line 3 = CF line 40 = 7,564M.
   - DHR 0000313616-26-000062: BS line 3 = CF line 46 = 4,615M.
10. **NetIncomeLossAvailableToCommonStockholdersBasic is net_income when NetIncomeLoss is absent and the income statement prints no preferred-dividend line.**
    - Seen at SCCO, ETN, BKNG, PH and WELL (0000766704-26-000010 IS lines 23-25).
    - ProfitLoss there includes NCI. Exclude it.
11. **Bank revenue = net interest income + non-interest income.** COF (0000927628-26-000024) prints no "total net revenue" line. Bank template.
12. **Interest line signs.** VRTX (0000875320-24-000062 IS line 18) prints "Interest expense" tagged InterestIncomeExpenseNet, with value -44.1M, below a separate "Interest income" line.
    - It is gross interest expense under a net tag: map with sign -1.
    - The tag map needs a sign column.
13. **Component sums seen:**

    - BKNG D&A: DepreciationNonproduction + AmortizationOfIntangibleAssets (0001075531-19-000009 CF lines 4-5).

15. **"Repurchase of ... common shares and other" with preferred redemptions on their own line is combined.**

## Definition calls, ruled (Dhairya 2026-11-11)

17. **Capitalized interest printed as a face reduction.** interest_expense = the expensed amount, i.e. the face interest line minus the face capitalized-interest line. Example: SCCO 416.7 - 47.4 = 369.3M (0001104659-26-021492 IS lines 9-10). This is the meaning of us-gaap InterestExpense, and the rule applies to every filer that prints it this way.
18. **Cash line that includes restricted cash on the face.** Serve the line as printed, labelled 'includes restricted cash'. Example: CB 0000896159-26-000005 BS line 9. No derived instant.
19. **Dividends or buybacks that include a subsidiary's exchangeable units.** Combined: a blank with reason, unless the equity note gives the common-only amounts, which are then mapped with evidence. Example: BLK 2025+ "Subco" lines, 0001193125-26-071966 CF lines 35 and 37.
