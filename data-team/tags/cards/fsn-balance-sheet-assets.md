# Card family: Balance-sheet asset tags, learned from FSN (written 2026-11-11 by the Tag Expert)

Founder order 2026-11-11: "FSN FOR LEARNING - which tag is what. SEC FOR MAPPING." This card teaches what each asset tag is, per company, for the seed of `analytics.company_tag_map`. Machine-readable twin: `data-team/tags/library/fsn-balance-sheet-assets.csv`. Extends `cash.md` (not contradicted, one refinement in sec. 3 and 18) and `utility-regulatory-and-crypto.md`.

## 0. Method, scope, limits

- Population: the 1,000 companies in `fsn.company_load` (`core.company` ids), filings with `fsn.filing.period >= 2015-01-01`, 10-K and 10-Q (incl. /A). Counts are DISTINCT COMPANIES with the tag on a balance-sheet face line (`fsn.line.stmt='BS'`, `inpth=false`) unless stated. Label counts use the lower-cased, whitespace-normalised `plabel`.
- Family split by `core.company.sic_code`: bank 6000-6199 (55 cos; includes crypto treasuries, see sec. 2), broker/asset manager 6200-6299 (37), insurer 6300-6411 (55, includes insurance brokers), REIT 6798 (53), utility 4900-4999 (57), other (740), no SIC (3).
- Calc parent: `fsn.calc_arc` of each company's LATEST 10-K (1,000 filings), counted only where the parent and child are both on that filing's BS face.
- `fsn.line_map` state: same latest-10-K set.
- Official meaning: FASB us-gaap 2025 documentation labels (`us-gaap-doc-2025.xml`, read 2026-11-11 from a copy already in the session scratchpad; downloaded from xbrl.fasb.org by an earlier run). Period type and balance: `fsn.tag.iord` / `crdr` (from FSN tag.txt), which agree with the taxonomy.
- Filings opened on sec.gov 2026-11-11 (R pages, plus 3 primary HTML documents), at most 1 request per second with the declared User-Agent: 30 R pages and 3 documents, listed in sec. 19.
- Evidence classes: FILING-CHECKED = the R page or document was opened this run (or in a card cited by name); DATA-INFERRED = FSN tables; ASSUMED = not checked.
- Commands: `psql` (one connection, `statement_timeout` 120s; `pg_stat_activity` showed 15 backends, of which 5 were Supavisor app backends, the rest Supabase platform backends) with per-tag `\copy` of `fsn.line` joined to `fsn.tag`; `fsn.filing`, `core.company`; latest-10-K BS lines joined to `fsn.line_map`; `fsn.calc_arc` for those filings; sibling-tag year counts. Scripts and extracts are in the session scratchpad (not committed).

## 1. Cross-cutting findings (read these first)

1. **The printed label in FSN (`plabel`) can be wrong while the tag is right.** In 8 filings where the label disagreed with the tag, the tag and value were correct in 7: the filer's XBRL label linkbase was shifted by one row or swapped. The human-readable document prints the correct label. FILING-CHECKED: STX 0001137789-26-000309 R2 vs `stx-20261002.htm` (Goodwill 1,221 labelled "Other intangible assets"; DTA 1,161 labelled "Other assets, net"; OtherAssetsNoncurrent 383 labelled "Total Assets"; the 10-Q prints Goodwill 1,221 / Deferred income taxes 1,161 / Other assets, net 383); POWL 0000080420-23-000051 R3 vs `powl-20230930.htm` (DTA 17,064 labelled "Other assets", OtherAssetsNoncurrent 13,129 labelled "Deferred income taxes"; the document prints Deferred income taxes 17,064 / Other assets 13,129); EXLS 0001297989-23-000009 R2 (Goodwill 405,579 labelled "Other intangible assets, net", intangibles 53,315 labelled "Goodwill"); DY 0000067215-19-000043 R2 (Goodwill 325,749 labelled "Operating lease right-of-use assets"); HLI 0001302215-23-000079 R2 (Assets 2,706,821 labelled "Other assets"); TRU 0001552033-17-000027 R2 (AssetsCurrent 513.7 labelled with the PP&E label); JBHT 0001437749-26-005294 R2 (CCE 17,284 labelled "Current assets:"). Two more broken-label forms: PINS 0001506293-22-000099 R2 labels AR "#REF!" (also AYI, CMC, DELL, GWRE in FSN, DATA-INFERRED); FICO 0001628280-24-045719 R4 labels the noncurrent DTA "Deferred Income Taxes and Other Assets, Current".
   **Rule:** a label-tag conflict is resolved by the tag, its value and its calc/position, never by the label alone. The doc-02 rule "a label may demote a line" must not fire on a label that names a DIFFERENT line of the same statement; flag it `label_conflict` for review instead.
2. **The one true wrong tag found:** JBHT 0001437749-20-021952 R3 and `jbht20200930_10q.htm`: the noncurrent line "Goodwill and intangible assets, net" 192,648 is tagged `PrepaidExpenseAndOtherAssetsCurrent`; the real prepaid line (288,224) is `PrepaidExpenseCurrent`. A tag-only map would serve 192,648 as prepaid. Company quirk.
3. **Inventory, accounts receivable, goodwill and PP&E have catalogue concepts (`inventory` 71, `accounts_receivable` 77, `goodwill` 116, `ppe_net` 56 / `ppe_net_resolved` 257) but NO `fsn.concept_rule` on the BS.** In the latest 10-Ks every such face line is `unmapped` (Goodwill 574 lines, PPE 503, InventoryNet 403, AR 429). Only total assets, current assets and cash have BS rules (`bs.anchors_assets_total@1`, `bs.current_assets_total@1`, `bs.cash_pure_line@1`).
4. **27% of latest-10-K BS lines have no `fsn.line_map` row at all** (14,067 of 50,954 lines, 27.6%; e.g. Assets: 730 mapped, 270 no row). The newest 10-K of roughly a quarter of the companies was loaded after the last line-map pass, or was never mapped. DATA-INFERRED; the Engineering owner should confirm.
5. **Unclassified balance sheets: 175 of 1,000 latest 10-Ks print `Assets` but no `AssetsCurrent`:** banks 48, insurers 41, REITs 44, brokers/asset managers 28, other 11 (homebuilders DHI, LEN, TOL, PHM, NVR; DE, CNH, GATX, UHAL, INVH, TRNO), no-SIC BDCs 3 (ARCC, BXSL, CBC). None of the 175 prints a "total current assets" line under any other tag. These companies use `OtherAssets` (not `OtherAssetsNoncurrent`) for "Other assets" (PNC, TRV, PLD, FITB, DHI FILING-CHECKED; 194 companies use `OtherAssets` on the BS, DATA-INFERRED).
6. **SIC 6199 is not "bank" here.** The 7 "banks" that print current assets are crypto treasuries and fintech: IREN, WULF, COIN, HUT, MSTR, BMNR, CRCL. Insurance brokers (MRSH, AON, AJG, WTW, BRO), HUM and ERIE (SIC 63xx/6411) print classified balance sheets. Tower/data-centre/timber REITs (AMT, CCI, SBAC, EQIX, IRM, LAMR, WY) are classified. Sector templates must follow the face, not SIC (as `SECTOR-TEMPLATES-2026-11-01.md` sec. 1 says).
7. **Era breaks found** (DATA-INFERRED counts, filing examples FILING-CHECKED): PP&E plus finance-lease ROU tag from 2020 (sec. 8); DTA element change 2016-2020 (sec. 13); ROU assets from 2019 (sec. 12); CCRC from 2018 (sec. 4, `cash.md`).

## 2. Presentation-only elements: StatementTable, StatementLineItems, *Abstract, *Axis, *Domain, *Member

- **Official:** `fsn.tag` marks `StatementTable`, `StatementLineItems`, every `*Abstract`, `*Axis` (datatype axis) and `*Domain`/`*Member` (datatype member) as abstract (15 taxonomy versions each). Abstract elements cannot carry a fact. Axes and members describe the dimension of a fact; they are never the fact.
- **FSN usage (latest 10-Ks, BS):** 5,549 `*Abstract` lines, 1,403 `*Member`, 771 `*Axis`, 767 `*Domain`, 639 StatementLineItems, 636 StatementTable. R pages show them as headings with no value (AAPL R5 `AssetsCurrentAbstract` "Current assets:"; PNC R5 `AssetsAbstract`; MSFT parenthetical R5 `StatementOfFinancialPositionAbstract`; PLD R2 `srt:ConsolidatedEntitiesAxis` "Prologis, L.P. [Member]" as a column-block heading). FILING-CHECKED.
- **Our line_map is inconsistent:** the same element kinds are `excluded` in some filings and `unmapped` in others (BS abstracts: 230 excluded, 3,838 unmapped, 1,481 no row); `excluded` is used only with `{"why": "parenthetical"}`. A face abstract is `unmapped` with no reason.
- **Exceptions to the abstract flag:** 14 custom `*Member` lines are flagged non-abstract (ETR 0000065984-26-000174 R6/R7, the Entergy subsidiaries as registrant columns; BBY 0000764478-26-000009 R3 `DeferredRevenueMember`, `GiftCardMember`), and EVRG 0001711269-26-000017 R4 carries `LegalEntityAxis`/`EntityDomain` with abstract = false. So the rule must key on the name suffix and the datatype, not only on `abstract`.
- **Decision (one rule for all):** `excluded`, reason `presentation_element` ("heading, table, axis, domain or member: carries no value"), for any tag that is `StatementTable`, `StatementLineItems`, ends in `Abstract`, `Axis`, `Domain` or `Member`, or has datatype axis/member, standard or custom, every era. Its value is never sought in companyfacts. A Member that names a co-registrant (ETR, PLD L.P., EVRG) is a dimension for the Engineering registrant filter, not a line. This matches the Mac library (`presentation_element`, 3,179 rows) and the cloud decision for `SupplementalCashFlowElementsAbstract`. Evidence: FILING-CHECKED (R pages above).

## 3. Assets (us-gaap:Assets)

- **Official:** "Amount of asset recognized for present right to economic benefit." Instant, debit.
- **Usage:** 1,000 of 1,000 companies on the BS face, all families. Labels (BS face, cos): "total assets" 996, "assets" 33, "assets, total" 18, "total" 10, footnoted variants ("total assets (1)" BKR, KIM; "(a)" GE, EXC; "(note 2)" IMO), "total consolidated assets" CDE. prole totalLabel in 41,437 of 41,682 lines.
- **Odd labels:** HLI "other assets" (label shift, the value is total assets, sec. 1). Non-face uses (DATA-INFERRED): AFG "assets of managed investment entities", SEIC "assets of consolidated variable interest entities", segment notes ("segment assets" CAT, "identifiable assets" GATX, TFC): these are dimensioned or note facts, not the consolidated total. There are 1,589 more Assets lines than filings (43,260 lines, 41,671 filings): VIE parentheticals, parent-only, segment.
- **Calc:** root of the asset side; in 8 latest 10-Ks it is also a child of `AssetsNet` in a note tree (ACGL, TROW, OHI, FNF, ...) and of `LiabilitiesAndStockholdersEquity` with weight -1 at PFGC and IBKR (filer-built identity trees). DATA-INFERRED.
- **Decision:** map -> `total_assets_resolved` (existing rule `bs.anchors_assets_total@1`). Sign as filed (positive). Only the undimensioned consolidated fact on the BS face; for multi-registrant filings (ETR, PLD/L.P., SO subsidiaries) the parent registrant's column only.
- **Evidence:** FILING-CHECKED AAPL 0000320193-25-000079 R5 (359,241M), PNC 0000713676-26-000020 R5 (573,572M), TRV 0000086312-26-000065 R5, PLD 0001193125-26-051453 R2 (two registrant blocks, same 98,724,256K), SO, MA, XOM, FITB, DHI, MRSH, STX.

## 5. CashAndCashEquivalentsAtCarryingValue (CCE)

- **Official:** "Amount of cash and cash equivalent. Cash includes ... currency on hand, demand deposit ...; cash equivalent includes short-term, highly liquid investment ..." Instant, debit.
- **Usage:** 961 companies in any statement; 935 on the BS face (banks only 20 of 55, insurers 41, REITs 52, utilities 57); 875 also use it on the cash flow (the beginning/ending balance where there is no restricted cash or before 2018). 528 distinct BS labels: "cash and cash equivalents" 885 cos, "cash and equivalents" 33, "cash" 28, "cash and cash equivalents, at carrying value" 26, "total cash and cash equivalents" 7, "cash and temporary cash investments" (ED, KR, NFG, VLO), "cash and invested cash" (LNC), "cash and cash investments" (MAS).
- **Confusable labels (perimeter):** "cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash" on CCE at AGCO, CSGP; THG "includes restricted cash of $106.1"; ESS, RHP "cash and cash equivalents - unrestricted"; DD/DOW VIE-restricted amounts in the label. Z (Zillow) 0001617640-24-000070 uses CCE on an "amortized cost" row (a note, DATA-INFERRED).
- **Calc parent:** `AssetsCurrent` 741, `Assets` 152 (unclassified), `CashCashEquivalentsAndShortTermInvestments` 7, CCRC 3.
- **Decision:** map -> `cash_and_equivalents_resolved` (`bs.cash_pure_line@1`, priority 10), as `cash.md`. Require USD (IVDA quirk). Where the label says restricted cash is included (AGCO, CSGP, THG), serve it as printed with a perimeter label (same ruling as CB, sec. 6).
- **Evidence:** FILING-CHECKED AAPL R5 (35,934M), PLD R2, SO R8, MA R5 (10,566M with restricted 561M on its own line), XOM R5, STX, DHI R2, MRSH R5, AMZN FY2019/FY2020 R6; plus the 7 filers in `cash.md`.

## 6. CashCashEquivalentsRestrictedCashAndRestrictedCashEquivalents (CCRC)

- **Official:** "Amount of cash and cash equivalent, and cash and cash equivalent restricted to withdrawal or usage; attributable to continuing operation." Instant, debit. Element in FSN from the 2017 taxonomy.
- **Usage:** 951 companies, almost all on the cash flow (950 cos, 53,393 lines). On the BS face: 132 companies (other 96, utility 9, REIT 9, insurer 8, bank 8, broker 2), rising from 5 (2017) to 91 (2024). BS labels: "cash and cash equivalents" 88 cos, "cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash and restricted cash equivalents" 19, "cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash" 18, "total cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash" 4, **"cash and due from banks" FITB and HWC**, "cash and invested cash" LNC, "cash and restricted cash" ELS, WWD/MKSI/REG "including restricted cash of $x".
- **Calc parent (BS):** `AssetsCurrent` 57, `Assets` 21.
- **Two BS forms:** (a) the face cash line itself (GILD, DHR per the cloud notes; CMI in `cash.md`; FITB); (b) a printed subtotal of CCE + restricted cash lines (DHI R2: 2,985.4 + 47.9 = 3,033.3, FILING-CHECKED).
- **Decision (applies the ruling "cash incl. restricted is the source only when no plain cash line is printed; CB served as printed with a label"):** on the BS, map -> `cash_and_equivalents_resolved` ONLY when no CCE / `Cash` line is printed on the same face; when the label or the face shows restricted cash inside (CB, ELS, WWD, MKSI), serve as printed with the perimeter label "incl. restricted cash". When CCE is printed (DHI form b), the CCRC line is `excluded` (subtotal that includes restricted cash). On the cash flow it is the ending-cash concept (no CF concept exists for it in the catalogue today; out of this card).
- **Exception:** banks whose face cash line is "cash and due from banks" tagged CCRC (FITB, HWC): see sec. 18 item 4.
- **Era:** 2018+ (ASU 2016-18); before that the same printed line is CCE.
- **Evidence:** FILING-CHECKED FITB 0000035527-26-000202 R2 (4,374M "Cash and due from banks", no CCE line), DHI 0000882184-25-000081 R2; CF usage FILING-CHECKED in `cash.md` (AAPL, AMZN, HTZ, AX).

## 11. InventoryNet

- **Official:** "Amount after valuation and LIFO reserves of inventory expected to be sold, or consumed within one year or operating cycle, if longer." Instant, debit.
- **Usage:** 572 companies (other 532, utility 32, REIT 4, insurer 2, bank 1, broker 1).
