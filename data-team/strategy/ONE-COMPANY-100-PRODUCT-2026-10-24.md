## 1. What a "100% page" for one company is
**Definition.** For company X, every cell of its template, for every period in scope, is in exactly one of the 8 frozen states with its evidence, and no cell is `defect`. 100% coverage = zero defects; 100% correct = every shown value passes the acceptance test in section 2. Nothing else is claimed.
- **Data points.** The table holds 163 per company (MEASURED, coverage table). The economic page is 133 lines after the F-1 removal of 30 duplicate/internal rows (approved under delegation, hash 57d3e3f337af7caa), over `catalog_version` (the sector rows are not final: doc 50). A corporate such as AAPL uses the corporate template; cells outside it are `not_in_template` and not shown.
- **Cell states and the wording the user reads** (register section 1): `value` (shown with its filing link); `derived` (shown with formula and inputs); `evidenced_zero` ("No dividends paid in the last 12 months"); `undefined_by_math` ("Not meaningful: negative earnings"); `history_horizon` ("History shorter than N years: first reported fiscal year FY<x>", or the 2015 floor wording `state.history.floor_2015`, still unfrozen, AMD 9); `not_presented` ("Not reported separately by this company" / "Reported combined with <label>" / the single "Not disclosed by this company", Verifier-confirmed); `not_in_template` (not shown); `defect` ("Not available yet"; must be ZERO before the certificate is issued).
- **Periods since 2015-01-01** (base `period_start` rule, F-2): fiscal years; the four quarters (Q4 from the filed note for fiscal years ending before 2021-08-09 [DATA-INFERRED, rule FILING-CHECKED], otherwise derived only under the additive allowlist; EPS and DPS never derived); balance-sheet instants; TTM where derivable; per-share values on ONE share basis after splits.
- **Explicitly NOT in the claim.**
  1. Cells whose definition awaits the named accountant (the AC cards: finance leases in debt, capitalized software in capex, D&A/capex composition, DPS declared vs paid, 53-week treatment, restatement basis, bank/insurer/REIT revenue). They are shown "Definition pending" and listed on the certificate; they are NOT counted as covered. Which cards touch the company is the Standardization Lead's call (ASSUMED: several, even for a non-financial large-cap).
  2. Any period before 2015-01-01.
  3. Correctness of the filer's own filing (a filer error is recorded as a company-scoped exception, never "fixed").
  4. Market data (price, market cap) beyond the checks in section 2; vendor data of any kind.
  5. A statement that the data are "100% correct": the supportable wording is "checked against the filing; error rate published".

## 2. The acceptance test (so the founder can see it pass)
Pass = ALL of A-F true, recorded in one file per company, tied to one code commit id, one rules/definitions version and one data watermark.
| # | Test | Evidence and source | Who | Zero-wrong rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Zero `defect` cells; every non-value state carries its evidence row | query over `cell_state` + `cell_evidence` (counts by state printed) | Triage (computes), Verifier re-runs | any defect fails |
| B | Headline census: 11 cells (revenue, operating income, net income, diluted EPS, total assets, total equity, CFO, capex, shares, total debt, market cap) x latest FY and latest quarter = 22 cells opened in the original filing (accession + R-file row) | SEC filing, independent reader on 10% (Reader 2) | Verifier not involved in building | 0 wrong of 22 |
| C | Reconciliation by code: every FY and quarter value since 2015 for the headline concepts equals the independent parse of the same filings (SEC Financial Statement Data Sets `num.txt`/`pre.txt`) within rounding, or the difference is explained in writing | FSDS, never our tables | Data Engineer (job), Verifier (reads differences) | 0 unexplained differences |
| D | History sample: one filing per fiscal year since 2015 (11), 10 randomly drawn cells each = 110 cells, plus every split year and every fiscal-year-change year checked in full | filings; seeded draw recorded | Verifier | 0 wrong of 110 (zero in 110 bounds the failure rate under 2.7% at 95% [DATA-INFERRED arithmetic]) |
| E | Invariants I1-I11 clean over all stored values of the company; each rule that fires has MEASURED precision (not a rule that gates on unmeasured precision) | certify run, rule precision file | Data Engineer, Verifier | no open blocking finding |
| F | Visible-reason check: every non-`value` cell shows the right wording; hidden-by-mistake count for every guard that touched the company is reported (target 0) | page screenshot + the guard log | Verifier + me (screenshot standing rule) | any correct value hidden by mistake is fixed before the certificate |
Any wrong row anywhere fails the company: the flag goes off, the family returns to its owner, the test restarts (Release Manager rule, doc 49 section 4).
**The proof we publish:** a per-company certificate page: company, cohort and group, code commit, rules/definitions version, data watermark, date; cell counts by state; the 22 headline cells with filing links; the reconciliation coverage; the Verifier sample table (n, wrong, bound); the list of "Definition pending" cells (should be empty); known filer-error exceptions; and the sentence "checked against the filing; our published error rate". The founder passes it by reading that page and opening three filing links of his choice. (Format is a product proposal; the page does not exist yet.)

## 4. The path 1 -> 10 -> 1,000
- **1 company:** every mechanism once, with full Verifier time (ASSUMED 1-2 Verifier-days for the test in section 2). Output: the certificate generator, the acceptance script, and the list of generic defects found.
- **10 companies = the pilot 10** (NVDA, AAPL, GOOG, MSFT, AMZN, META, AVGO, TSLA, LLY, MU): already studied, seven remain to re-run; they exercise splits, multi-class shares, successor CIK (AVGO), dimensioned share counts, pre-revenue reason (LLY). Reused from company #1: identity/P1, authority gate, split re-base, reason store and rules, state machine, certificate generator. New per company: company-scoped vocabulary, any overlay, the AC cards it touches.
- **1,000 (cohort v1.1):** reused: everything above plus templates; new per company TYPE: the template and sector rows (bank, insurer, REIT, BDC, pre-revenue, utility variants), the accountant cards for that type, detector audits (30 per group). Batches of 10 with the loop: run suite -> classify new defects into rule families -> fix by rule -> re-run -> Verifier -> certify.
- **KPIs per batch:** companies certified; NEW defect classes found per batch (must fall toward zero; if a batch finds new classes at the same rate as the first, the "one-time solution" test fails); cost per certified company in Verifier minutes and engineer hours (must fall); rule-closed share of defects (must rise); hidden-by-mistake count; certificate pass rate at first attempt.
- **Launch v0 vs the 100% goal (proposal for the founder):** the 100% goal stays the promise for the 1,000. Launch v0 becomes "the first N certified companies are public, with the certificate page, and every other company is labelled unverified", N chosen by pass rate (ASSUMED N = 10), instead of waiting for all 1,000. This lets users and the "does trust sell" hypothesis (doc 43 risk) be tested at company 10, not 1,000. The founder decides.

## 6. Honest confidence and the top 5 risks
**Confidence (ASSUMED, not measured):** about 50% that AAPL passes A-F within 6 weeks of the clock starting if the accountant is named and the push path works; about 75% for a simpler company; below 25% for any company if neither is resolved. Reasons: two of three generic fixes exist on branches (split re-base COMPLETE, reason rules PARTIAL), the third (total debt) failed its gate four times, certification precision is 57% strict so Verifier time, not automation, carries the test, and nothing is on main.
**Top 5 risks**
1. **Total debt (root fix #1) keeps failing the Verifier gate** (V1-V4) and AAPL's own known error is a total-debt error. Test: V5 sample of the carrying-lines fix passes on AAPL and 3 peers before the company test starts.
2. **No accountant:** even a non-financial large-cap touches several AC cards; without rulings its certificate is not 100%. Test: the Standardization Lead lists the cards that touch company #1 and each has a ruling or a visible pending state.
3. **No stable release candidate / push path blocked:** the certificate would describe code that is not deployed. Test: one commit id on origin/main produced the numbers (state snapshot shows it).
4. **Certification precision too low to automate** (57% strict, 19% review, round 3): the acceptance test falls on manual Verifier time and a second reader. Test: round 4 precision on held-out shards, per rule, before any rule gates.
5. **Reading approach undecided (API values vs the filing's own trees, hybrid design; as-filed pilot on 10 running):** if the filing route changes the mapping, results move. Test: the pilot's disagreement count (cells the filing route fixes vs breaks) on AAPL before the company test starts.
