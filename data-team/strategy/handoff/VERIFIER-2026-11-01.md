Method lessons:
- Match rows by XBRL element (defref_<prefix>_<Tag> in the R page HTML), not label: utility pages print "Total" several times.
- Combined co-registrant R pages print the PARENT block first, then the subsidiary's. Read the subsidiary block, not the first match.
- A FSN pre line plus a num value is not "on the face". Check the statement has that period column (10-K quarters sit in the Quarterly Data note; 10-Q cash flows are year-to-date).
- 52/53-week years: match columns by end date within 3-10 days AND months; the kit gives months=None on some annual pages (AMD), so read raw.
- parse_rpage gaps: currency-prefixed cells ("CAD 0.5625") parse to None; a mixed "$ in Thousands, EUR in Millions" header gives no columns. Read raw <tr> rows in those cases.
- Outflows print in parentheses; positive tag values are right. Derived quarters: check FY minus 9M from the two printed lines.
- Always run a positive control when a scan returns "nothing found" (it caught nothing wrong here, but it is the only proof the scan works).
- Pre-split values: look for a 10x/5x step in the series even without a split-ratio fact (SHOP had none).
- Restated figures: the later filing's comparative column is the restated basis; production may keep the original.
