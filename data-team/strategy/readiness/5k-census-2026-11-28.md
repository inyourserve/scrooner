Read-only snapshot of the dev database, taken 2026-11-28 to 29 (one connection, read-only transaction, 300 s statement cap). No writes, no SEC requests. Per-company rows are in [`5k-census-2026-11-28.csv`](5k-census-2026-11-28.csv).

**Universe.** 5,192 `core.company` rows with status `active`, ranked by the latest `analytics.company_screening_snapshot.market_cap` (848 have no market cap and sort last). The 1,000 cohort is `doc/data-moat/team/top1000/shards.csv` (1,011 of its 1,014 company ids are active). `core.universe_member` is stale (138 primary eligible, snapshot 2026-08-21). `pipeline-fsn-load-bulk.yml` (76d1993f) reads `doc/data-moat/team/top1000/universe_5000.csv`, but that file isn't on origin/main yet. When it lands, rejoin this census to it on `company_id`.

**Shown cell.** An `analytics.canonical_fact` row on or after 2015-01-01 with a fiscal period, for one of the 53 concepts the proof covers. It is matched to `cell_proof` on company, concept, grain, period end and (for durations) period start. FY+I means the annual and instant grain: the grain that `cell_display_state` and certificates cover today.

## Known blockers, checked

- **Coverage and display state hard-wired to ranks 1-100: CONFIRMED.** `mapper/top100_coverage.py` has `load_top(path, n=100)`, `main(..., n=100)` and `select_companies` (only ids in that list). `pipeline/scripts/top100_coverage.py` never passes `n`. `shards.csv` stops at rank 1,000, and helper loaders read `resolver-all110-*`. Result: 96 companies have display state; ranks 101+ have 0.
- **44,871 FY+I DISAGREE across ranks 101-1000: CONFIRMED, within 0.3%.** Today it is 44,718 on the 897 ranked rows, or 45,486 including the 14 unranked S101 backfill rows. 97% of it is `restated_copies_disagree` (one family, so one rule fix).
- **Proof runs serial per company: PARTLY.** Within a process, `scrooner-proof run` loops companies one by one (`jobs/proof.py`). `pipeline-cells-proof.yml` already fans out to a 20-shard matrix (`max-parallel: 20`), and the last proof run covered 996 companies in November. Proof speed is not the binding constraint. Its inputs are: 4,190 companies have no FSN and 892 have no resolver CSV.

## First 3 engineering changes, by cells unblocked

1. **Ingest FSN for ranks 1,001-5,000, plus the September/October 10-Ks, through one universe file.** Commit `universe_5000.csv` from the census ranking. Run `pipeline-fsn-load-bulk.yml` by rank range, and turn on `FSN_NIGHTLY_STAGE` so new 10-Ks land. This unblocks 4.37M + 72k cells (blockers 1 and 7) and is the precondition for everything else.
2. **Make resolve and coverage/display-state rank-agnostic.** Generate resolver CSVs for every FSN company from the same `resolve_all.py` path, not the 110 list. Replace `load_top(shards, 100)` and `resolver-all110` with a company-set input (the universe file), and make the proof claim set equal the shown concept set, so no served cell goes without a proof row. This covers blockers 2, 3 and 6: 1.34M + 486k cells, and display state for 906 companies.
3. **Fix the `restated_copies_disagree` family and give `total_debt_resolved` a proof path.** One rule: pick the authoritative restated copy under the one-vintage rule (X87). Add a composite debt proof from the parts contract instead of UNCHECKED. Together these clear 72,690 DISAGREE and about 28,745 FY+I UNCHECKED cells across ~870-990 companies, about 95% of all FY+I DISAGREE+UNCHECKED (73,186 of 77,148).

Method: `census.py` (session scratch, not committed). It uses set-based queries batched by 400 company ids, about 6 minutes in total.
