## Root cause (print FILING-CHECKED by Verifier 2; code path DATA-INFERRED)
CSGP (company 2904) FY2023 net income is printed 374.7 ($M, one decimal), unit 100,000; the 9M input is printed in thousands. The quarter 96,475,000 claims precision the FY does not have. resolve_scale already shows a quarter at the coarsest PRINTED unit (scale x 10^-decimals), which gives 96.5M. The miss was upstream: `_scale_suspect` chose which quarters to look up from trailing zeros in whole-million classes only (max >= 1e6). 374,700,000 is a multiple of 1e5, not 1e6, so it never reached the printed-unit check.

## Fix (general, no company special case)
`_scale_suspect` compares the largest power of ten dividing each input (1e9 down to 10) and selects the cell when the coarsest is >= 1e4 and coarser than the finest. Trailing zeros still only SELECT; the unit always comes from the filing. Superset of the old test, so every old candidate is still looked up.
