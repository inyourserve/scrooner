## 5. Three ways this program fails to move the needle, with a test each
1. **Denominator theatre.** The largest workstream (57% of blanks) changes reasons and denominators, not values, so the headline rises while users see no more correct numbers. Test: weekly on the fixed 986-company, 160,718-cell denominator, report value-filled cells, current-and-not-stale cells, and FC-confirmed wrong values removed, from step 0's measured baseline. If value-filled and current cells do not rise while the applicable-denominator figure does, the program is failing; say so in the founder report.
2. **Building on projections and an unproven instrument.** Every number in doc 40 is a projection from a market-cap-ordered 45% sample, and certify's `wrong_value` has measured false positives and misses (data-team/36). Test: by day 7, step 0 has run (measured split with watermark) and certify precision per invariant is known from 30 Verifier-checked flags each. If either is missing on day 7, stop new builds and escalate capacity. If an invariant fails K3, fix it before it gates certification.
3. **Infrastructure before visible fixes.** Structure tables, composition schema and new invariants absorb the month while built, filing-checked guards stay held and users still see the NVDA, Visa, JPM and MSFT wrong values. Test: by day 10, ranks 1, 2 and 4 are committed, live and dry-run-reviewed, and the FC wrong-value list from data-team/36 (JPM 5 cells, Visa FY DPS, NVDA about 216 cells, MSFT growth) shows each cell as either corrected or hidden with a reason on the company page.
