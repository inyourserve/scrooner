## Findings for the builder
1. **LYV:** an "Amortization" line on the intangible element (AmortizationOfIntangibleAssets...) is asset D&A; do not exclude it. The IS tie (449,976K) confirms.
2. **Favorable/unfavorable lease intangibles** (lessee side, OLLI) are lease cost like above/below-market leases; exclude them. Read the intangibles note when an "Amortization of intangibles" line exists and the only amortizing intangibles are leases.
3. **ROU lines:** a generic "Amortization of right-of-use assets" label counts as finance only if the label or the lease note says finance. The element alone is not enough (TXG, QXO). QXO's sum exceeding the IS D&A twice over is a tie-out failure that should hold.
4. **Over-holds:** marketable-securities premium/discount accretion and debt-issuance accretion are excluded items, not combined D&A lines (RVMD, PTGX). Reclassify them as excluded.
