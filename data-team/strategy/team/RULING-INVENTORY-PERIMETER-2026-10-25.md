# Ruling (PROPOSAL): inventory perimeter for quick_ratio, 2026-10-25

Standardization Lead. Status: **proposed**, not approved; it needs the approver agent (not the preparer) and the Verifier. Affects `analytics.metric_definition` 42 `quick_ratio` = (Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liabilities, and the canonical `inventory` concept (today only `us-gaap:InventoryNet`, `mapper/expanded_concepts.py` L284), which also feeds inventory_days, the cash conversion cycle and the inventory reconciliation. Asked by: VERIFY-QUICK-RATIO-2026-10-25 item 3.

## Recommendation
1. **Yes. For the quick ratio, deduct materials and supplies, spare parts, fuel and gas in storage when each is a CURRENT face line.** The quick ratio asks what can pay current liabilities without selling or using up operating stock. Materials and supplies are never sold. They are used up in maintenance and construction, so they turn into cash more slowly than merchandise. Whether ASC 330 calls them inventory does not change the fact that they are not quick assets. Including them is also the only rule we can apply the same way everywhere. WEC presents one undivided line, "Materials, supplies, and inventories". A rule that excluded materials would have to split a number the filer never splits.
2. **The canonical `inventory` concept = the filer's total current inventories as shown on the face.** It is net of valuation and LIFO reserves, and it equals the sum of every current face line the filer presents under its inventories caption or tags with an inventory element. Precedence: (a) a face total line when one is printed (InventoryNet, or a total row such as MKC "Total inventory"); (b) otherwise the sum of the face sub-lines (XOM), checked against the printed current-assets total. A total is never added to its own parts. Only lines in the face statement role count; note breakdowns (InventoryFinishedGoodsNetOfReserves, InventoryRawMaterialsAndSupplies in a note) are never added on top.
3. **One exception the taxonomy forces: `us-gaap:MaterialsSuppliesAndOther` is not inventory.** Its FASB definition (printed in CP's own R4) reads "capitalized costs of materials, supplies, and other assets, **which are not included in inventory** but used in production". Keep it out of `inventory`, so that inventory_days and the reconciliation keep the filer's own classification. Add a component concept `materials_supplies_not_inventory`, current face line only, and have quick_ratio **version 2** subtract both: (CA - inventory - materials_supplies_not_inventory) / CL. Condition: the face label must name only stock items (materials, supplies, parts, fuel). If the label adds receivables or any other quick item, quarantine the cell (COMBINED_LINE_ONLY).
4. **Exclusions:** noncurrent inventory (InventoryNoncurrent; WES NGL line-fill; long-term ore stockpiles); a supplies figure that is not its own face line (VAL: inside "other current assets"); contract assets, costs in excess of billings, emission allowances and crypto, unless the filer prints them inside the inventories caption. A combined face line that mixes inventory with quick items (for example "receivables and inventories") makes the cell unavailable (COMBINED_LINE_ONLY), never a guess. Real-estate inventory counts as inventory where the balance sheet is classified; unclassified balance sheets get no quick ratio (existing rule).
