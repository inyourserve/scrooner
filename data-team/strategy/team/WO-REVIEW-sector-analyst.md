### 1.3 Group assignment v2: the rule, stated once
Rule `group.assign@2` (replaces S2 s3 wording and doc 47 WO-4.1 wording):
1. **The group is decided by the filer's own face statements** (latest 10-K balance sheet and income statement; for filers without a 10-K, the latest 10-Q). Signature lines per group are listed in S2 s3 (bank: deposits + loans + net interest income; insurer: premiums + losses/benefits + reserves; BDC: total investment income + net assets; equity REIT: rental revenue + real estate at cost; and so on).
2. **SIC is the second, independent signal and the trigger.** Agreement of SIC bin and a signature of at least two face lines assigns the group by code. Disagreement, a lone flag, a blank SIC, or any of SIC 6411, 6199, 6211, 6200, 6282, 4610, 492x sends the filing to a human read. Neither a mapped data value (the `bdc_total_investment_income` failure: 24 of 40 insurers) nor a vendor field may be a signal.
3. **Overlays, not extra groups,** for: pre_revenue, royalty_land, captive_finance (PCAR, CAT, DE, F, GM: unopened), conglomerate_insurer (L, MKL, BRK-A if admitted), insurance_operations (KKR, APO, AMP), managed_care_operations (CVS), crypto_holdings. An overlay adds COND cells to the group's template and never removes REQ cells of the group.
4. **Hybrid precedence:** the group is the one that owns the face revenue line (largest revenue caption on the income statement in the latest fiscal year); the second business becomes an overlay when its signature (at least two lines) is on the face. Both are recorded with the accession and the lines.
5. **Effective dating:** every row carries `group`, `overlays[]`, `effective_from`, `evidence_fy` (the fiscal year whose statements were read), `evidence_accession`, `history_status` in {`verified_from_<FY>`, `unverified_before_<FY>`}. v0 materializes only the latest period (register s2), so history states for FYs before `evidence_fy` are derived on read using the group in force and flagged `unverified` until the per-FY break detector (AT-4.1j) has run; nothing is stored under an unverified assumption. Keys are `company_id` (not ticker, not CIK alone, to survive PSKY/SKYD, EQR/VMRK, XOM successor CIK).
6. **Excluded is a group with reasons:** `subsidiary_registrant` (EAI, and the 8 subsidiary rows Gate A found outside the cohort: Georgia Power 613, SoCalGas 1080, SCE 1079, Consumers 1165, Ameren Illinois 257, EIDP 351, NSTAR 211, PSNM 1044, which T3 will otherwise inherit), `no_10k_yet` (PS, HONA, INIO, ARXS, JAN), `successor_cik_pending` (XOM), `acquired_or_deregistered` (WBD, AVB id 1895), `non_screener` (SPAC, pool trust, ABS), `foreign_private_issuer`.

Group count on the 986 (S2 s3, DI beyond the 258 opened): corporate 707, equity REIT 54, bank 44, insurer 40, utility 32, energy 31, midstream 14, asset manager 14, broker-dealer 11, crypto/digital 8, managed care 7, insurance broker 7, exchange 6, non-bank lender 4, excluded 3, BDC 2, mortgage REIT 2.
