## 3. Method card (deliverable 2): share basis for market cap and per-share price ratios

### 3.1 Concept contract `market_cap` (proposed v2; NA-15 + NA-16)
- **Economic definition:** the market value of the issuer's outstanding residual-equity securities at the price date d: the sum over equity classes c of shares outstanding(c) x price(c, d), with all quantities in the unit of the priced security.
- **Statement / type:** derived metric; inputs are instants (share counts) and a market price. USD, `Decimal` end to end.
- **Attribution:** registrant-level (the CIK's own cover). Shared-accession and combined-registrant filings follow the Gate A identity rules (ledger rows 83 and 85).
- **Outstanding** = issued minus treasury. Never weighted averages, never diluted counts, never pre-funded warrants, options or convertibles (perimeter questions are in AC-22 to AC-25).

### 3.2 Share-count source precedence (per class)
1. Cover `dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding` of the latest periodic filing (10-K, 10-Q, their amendments, 10-KT; never 8-K, DEF 14A or S-1), **read from the filing instance**, because companyfacts strips the class dimension (doc 21 section 8.7; ledger row 44). One fact per class member; the undimensioned fact is used only when the filing has no class-dimensioned cover facts.
2. Balance-sheet `CommonStockSharesOutstanding` (instant, after treasury) of the same or a later periodic filing, per class, when the cover is missing or states "including treasury shares" (DBVT).
3. **Never** `WeightedAverageNumberOf*SharesOutstanding*` as the value. WASO is a plausibility check only. This reverses today's `_choose_shares` fallback and its "prefer WASO when the cover differs by more than 25%" branch (RPAY, TBLA). Impact not measured: Comcast, UPS, Ford and Mastercard use the WASO branch today (code comment, price_metrics.py). They need the per-class cover reader before the switch, or they turn hidden.
4. Never an ADR-member cover fact (finding 7). Never a class with no economic rights in residual equity (RPAY Class V).
5. **Freshness:** the share count's as-of date must be on or after the latest periodic filing's period end (existing `MAX_SHARES_AGE_DAYS` = 480 stays). The ratio date rules in 3.3 apply as well.

### 3.3 NA-15 `mcap.ads_conversion@1` (arithmetic; no accountant)
1. **Detect:** the listing priced for the company is a depositary share when the cover `dei:Security12bTitle` row whose `dei:TradingSymbol` equals the priced ticker contains "Depositary" (AKTX, ZLAB, DBVT, GPCR, AMRN, SLN, SCNI, KZIA, TEVA). `core.listing.security_type = 'ADR'` is NOT the detector: it is set for multi-listing companies only (8 companies; AMRN and DBVT among them, not AKTX, ZLAB, GPCR or SLN; DI).
2. **Ratio:** parse "each (ADS) representing (the right to receive) N ordinary share(s)" from that `Security12bTitle`. Accept digits ("80,000", "10", "4,000"), number words ("three", "five", "twenty (20)", "five-hundred") and fractions ("one-half (1/2)", historical DBVT). Anything else: hide `ads_ratio_unparsed`. Store the ratio with its accession, context and `effective_from` (the ratio-change date when stated, else the filing's period end).
3. **Ratio currency:** the ratio must come from the latest periodic filing filed on or before d, and must not be superseded. Hide `ads_ratio_changed_since_source` when (a) that filing's text announces a ratio change not yet effective on its filing date (AKTX 10-K -013664 announced the 80,000 ratio, effective on or after 2026-03-31, while its cover still read 2,000), or (b) any F-6, F-6/A, F-6 POS or F-6EF (the depositary's registration amendments) is filed after it, or (c) an 8-K/6-K between the source filing and d announces a ratio change. AKTX timeline: 8-K 0001493152-26-010602 on 2026-03-17 (the announcement date the 10-K cites); 10-K -013664 filed 2026-03-30 with cover ratio 2,000 plus the announcement text (signal a); F-6/A 0000919574-26-001972 on 2026-03-31, the effective date (signal b); 10-Q -037730 on 2026-08-13 with cover 80,000 (EDGAR submissions index, form and date only; the 8-K and F-6/A texts were not opened). A cover ratio read from the newest periodic filing was wrong from 2026-03-31 to 2026-08-13. Signal (c) covers changes announced after the source filing and needs 8-K text reading (untested).
4. **Market cap** = ordinary shares outstanding (3.2) / ratio(d) x ADS price(d). Use the ratio in force on the price date. Ordinary counts are not changed by a pure ratio change (AMRN, KZIA, SCNI, DBVT, AKTX notes say so, FC). A share subdivision does change them (ZLAB 2022) and belongs to the split table (WO-22).
5. **Per-share ratios:** P/E = ADS price / (EPS per ordinary share x ratio), or market cap / NI attributable (AC-14 decides which). Dividend yield = DPS per ordinary share x ratio / ADS price. Where the filer also reports a per-ADS figure (AMRN supplementary "Net loss per ADS"; ZLAB FY2022 10-K per-ADS lines), use it as a cross-check, not as an extra source.
6. **History:** a historical price series needs the ratio in force on each price date. Ratio changes are reverse ADS splits for the price, so a split-adjusted price series pairs with today's ratio; an unadjusted one pairs with the ratio of its own date. Today `price_metrics.py` reads only the latest bar, so only the current ratio is needed now.

### 3.4 NA-16 `mcap.class_sum@1` (arithmetic for listed classes; the AC items cover the rest)
1. Enumerate the economic classes from the cover class members (3.2 step 1). If any member's count is missing, hide `class_count_incomplete`. Never pick one class as the issuer total (doc 21 section 8.7).
2. **Listed class:** count x that class's own price on d. `core.market_price_alpaca` holds one price per company, so a second listed class (GOOG/GOOGL, BRK.A/BRK.B, FOX/FOXA, NWS/NWSA, LEN/LEN.B, HEI/HEI.A, BF.A/BF.B) has no price today. **Engineering gap:** per-listing prices are needed (Data Engineer; migration). Until then, hide `class_price_missing` rather than reprice a class at another class's price.
3. **Different economic rights:** convert only with a ratio stated in the filing (BRK: "Class B share = 1/1,500 Class A", 10-K 0001193125-26-083899 R4 footnote, already FC on the tag card). With a stated ratio and one class priced, market cap = (A x 1,500 + B) x price(B), or the two classes at their own prices. With no stated ratio, hide.
4. **Unlisted class:** see AC-24. **Up-C units:** see AC-22.
5. **Perimeter match:** the earnings or book value in any ratio must cover the same holders as market cap. Class A only goes with NI attributable to the registrant (RPAY "Net loss attributable to the Company"). A fully exchanged market cap goes with NI including the NCI that the units represent. A mismatch is hidden `perimeter_mismatch`.

### 3.5 Plausibility guards (hide-only; keep lane 3's)
- `shares_scale_conflict` (200x against cover, instant median, weighted median): keep it, and note it catches scale errors only, never ADS.
- Add: chosen share count vs cover per-class sum outside 0.5x-2x gives `shares_cover_mismatch` (a tighter internal bound; not an error rate, a trigger to hide).
- Add: a WASO fact whose value / printed-statement EPS denominator implies EPS inconsistent with printed EPS by more than rounding gives `waso_scale_filer_error` (RPAY, TBLA). Data Engineer item; needs the printed EPS, from the filing instance.

### 3.6 Census to replace the hand list (Data Engineer; read-only)
For every company in the population, read `dei:Security12bTitle`, `dei:TradingSymbol` and the class-dimensioned `dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding` from the latest periodic filing's instance (about 5k fetches at the SEC limit, one fetch process; or the SEC Financial Statement and Notes data sets if their text table carries dei text facts [VERIFY]). Output: a per-company, per-class, per-accession table: priced-ticker class, depositary flag, ratio, counts and as-of dates. This both detects ADS issuers (SLN shows the list approach misses them) and supplies the per-class counts that 3.2 step 1 needs. Schema needs a migration, number claimed in doc 53.
