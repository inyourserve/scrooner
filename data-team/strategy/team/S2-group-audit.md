## 1. Method and limits
- Draft file: `data/group_assignment_draft.csv` (986 companies). Sample: 258 distinct companies, 256 had a 10-K to open (PS, XOM have none, see section 6). Each company: latest 10-K from the submissions JSON, FilingSummary.xml, then the balance sheet and income statement R-files (about 770 sec.gov requests, paced below 2 per second, declared User-Agent from pipeline/.env, about 64 MB, deleted afterwards). Classification by statement shape: row labels (deposits, loans, net interest income, premiums, reserves, total investment income, net assets, rental revenue, real estate, brokerage receivables, digital assets, cost of revenue, current assets).
- Sampling: groups of 30 or fewer were taken in full (insurer 28, bdc_fund 29, bank 40 and energy 34 and reit 54 and utility 50 were sampled at 30 by even spacing over CIK order). **Deviation: no reliable market cap exists in the data folder, so I could not stratify by market cap rank; spacing over CIK order is size-neutral, not size-stratified.** other_financial (49) and corporate (30 even-spaced) as follows: other_financial was opened in full. Corporate: 30 even-spaced plus 11 targeted (every corporate with SIC 6xxx or blank SIC except one that fell in the even sample).
- Duplicates in my sample list (BAC, ONB, HOMB, SF, AMG and others drawn twice) were counted once, so bank n=27, reit n=29, utility n=28, energy n=26.
- A clean sample is "none found in this sample", not a proven rate. Corporate was not opened beyond 41; the other 660 corporates are unchecked except by the SIC rule.
- My script's first income-statement pick was wrong for 29 companies (LNC, PGR, CB, ESNT, KNSL, RLI, WTW, SOFI, RKT, AFRM, IREN, GLXY, HUT, IBKR, RJF, ARES, AJG, EOG, DUOL, RGLD, TPL, EGP, WELL, ELS, AHR, LINE, AGNC, KKR, AON: statement named "Operations and Comprehensive Income" or an EPS note was taken). I re-opened the correct statement for all of them (second fetch) before classifying.

## 3. Corrected rule set (SIC + signals) applicable to all 986
Order matters; first match wins.
1. **Excluded** (section 6): EAI, PS, XOM by name until resolved.
2. **bank**: SIC 6021, 6022, 6035, 6036; or blank SIC with deposits + loans + NII on the face (CBC); override: AXP, SYF, SOFI (SIC 6199, but deposits + loans + interest income/expense + provision on the face).
3. **lender_nonbank**: SIC 6141, 6162 and no deposits: CACC, OMF, AFRM, RKT.
4. **bdc**: blank or fund SIC AND statement shows total investment income and net assets: ARCC, BXSL. Do NOT use `bdc_total_investment_income` has-value as the signal (it fires for insurers).
5. **managed_care**: SIC 6324 (HUM, UNH, CNC, ELV, MOH, CI, OSCR). Template is corporate-like (classified balance sheet) with premium revenue and medical costs.
6. **insurer**: SIC 6311, 6321, 6331, 6351, 6361, 6399, plus EQH and ACT (both SIC 6411). Signal check: income statement has premiums and benefits/losses; balance sheet has reserves/policy liabilities.
7. **insurance_broker**: SIC 6411 other than EQH, ACT: MRSH, BRO, AON, AJG, RYAN, WTW, ERIE. Corporate shape (commissions and fees, fiduciary assets).
8. **broker_dealer**: SIC 6211 and 6200 with securities borrowed/segregated cash/receivables from brokers: GS, MS, JEF, SF, RJF, SCHW, IBKR, SNEX, FRHC, LPLA, HOOD.
9. **asset_manager**: SIC 6282 plus BLK, SEIC (6211): BEN, IVZ, AMG, TROW, VCTR, BLK, SEIC, ARES, APO, BX, CG, TPG, KKR, AMP. Exceptions to corporate: MORN (research publisher), EVR, HLI (advisory, corporate-shaped).
10. **exchange**: ICE, CME, NDAQ, CBOE, TW, MKTX (classified balance sheet with clearing/restricted lines; corporate-shaped with sector lines).
11. **crypto_digital**: SIC 6199 with digital assets on the balance sheet: COIN, CRCL, GLXY, MSTR, IREN, HUT, WULF, BMNR (miners and MSTR have cost of revenue and classified balance sheets).
12. **reit_mortgage**: SIC 6798 with interest income and repo and no real estate: NLY, AGNC. **reit_equity**: SIC 6798, 6500 or 6510 with rental revenue and real estate: all other REITs including TRNO and INVH. Operating-REIT flag (classified balance sheet, service revenue): SBAC, AMT, CCI, EQIX, DLR, IRM, LAMR, RYN, WY, LINE, HST, RHP.
13. **midstream**: SIC 4922, 4923, 4924 (exporters), 4610: KMI, WMB, OKE, ET, EPD, MPLX, PAA, WES, TRGP, AM, DTM, CQP, LNG, VG. **utility**: SIC 4911, 4931, 4932, 4941, 4991 and the gas distributors ATO, NFG, SWX (and NI, UGI, WTRG, AWK). **Waste (SIC 4953, 4955): corporate.**
14. **energy**: SIC 1311, 1381, 1389, 2911, plus DINO (listed under 4610 but a refiner).
15. Everything else: **corporate**. Overlays, not groups: pre_revenue (PCVX; rule = no revenue line AND R&D line), royalty/land (RGLD, TPL, LB, VNOM), captive-finance (PCAR and similar; none opened), conglomerate-insurer (L, MKL).
Principle: SIC is the first key, but 6411, 6199, 6211, 6200, 6282, 4610/492x and blank SIC always need a statement-shape check; never let a data-value signal (such as `bdc_total_investment_income`) override SIC.
Result on 986: corporate 707, reit_equity 54, bank 44, insurer 40, utility 32, energy 31, midstream 14, asset_manager 14, broker_dealer 11, crypto_digital 8, managed_care 7, insurance_broker 7, exchange 6, lender_nonbank 4, excluded 3, bdc 2, reit_mortgage 2.
