## 1. In plain words

- **Most of these gaps are not mapping gaps.**
  - Of the 13,018 `no_mapped_line` dividend keys, 12,917 are filings whose cash-flow statement has no dividend line at all. Examples: Waters, Cadence, Copart, CarMax, which pay no dividends.
  - Of the 12,544 buyback quarter keys, about 7,300 are filings whose only repurchase-like line is one we exclude (shares withheld for taxes).
  - These keys need an evidenced reason, not more mapping.
- **The quarter gaps are not same-basis blocks.**
  - `pilot.py` counts a key as `quarter_not_printed_or_derivable` only when it has no cell at all.
  - A YTD subtraction blocked by SAME_BASIS writes a reasoned `defect` cell (`mixed_vintage_inputs` / `no_basis_witness`), which counts as reasoned. So none of the 19,170 quarter gap keys is a same-basis block.
- **The YTD inputs that are "on one basis but blocked" are an identity-check bug.**
  - 289 companies print the FX effect on cash under a standard tag the cash-flow identity check did not know. Nucor's tag is `EffectOfExchangeRateOnCash…IncludingDisposalGroupAndDiscontinuedOperations`.
  - Every cash-flow column of theirs therefore failed `cf_parts_eq_change`, and every CFO, buyback, dividend and D&A value in it was held. A held 6M/9M/FY then left its quarter with no cell.
  - Nucor FY2024: the parts sum to −2,813, FX is −16, and the printed change is −2,829, so the column ties exactly once FX is counted.
- **Real coverage gaps exist but are small.** A common buyback or dividend line under a tag no seed maps covers 1,598 keys. Top tags: `PaymentsForRepurchaseOfEquity` (500), `PaymentsOfCapitalDistribution` (163), custom tags.

## 2. How this was measured

1. **Every key classified.** I scanned the cash-flow face of every filing for the 941 gap companies in all 59 FSN sets, straight from sec.gov (`fsn_sec_cf_faces.py`). Each gap key was then classified by what its filing prints, using run 7's own line classifiers (`cf_gap_classify.py`). Results: `pipeline/scripts/slot_records/results/cf_gaps_2026-11-02/`.
2. **The store reproduced locally.** I picked 15 companies, the top ones of each class, and built their company directories from sec.gov. I loaded them into a scratch Postgres 16 with every migration and the committed seeds, and ran the pilot steps locally.
   - **The local baseline reproduces the cohort run exactly** for these 15 companies: dividends 255 / 331 / 48 and buybacks 231 / 323 / 37 gap keys by cause.
   - Each change was then measured on top of the previous one.
