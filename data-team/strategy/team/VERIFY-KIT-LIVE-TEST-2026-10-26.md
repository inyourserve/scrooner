# Verify: verifier kit live test (origin/verifier-kit e7fa26c), 2026-10-26

Verifier 2. I ran the kit's `filing_kit.py` (unmodified copy, my own rate-limited client with SEC_USER_AGENT; 124 SEC requests) on 25 balance-sheet instants whose total liabilities I had already derived by hand (RYN, EPD, BBIO, AWK x3, DUK x2, JPM, KHC, DTE x2, MHK, HRL x2, CAH, IDACORP x2, LDOS, TYL, XEL, VMI, BSX, LAMR). FILING-CHECKED.

**Result: 0 of 25 as shipped. 9 of 25 once I fixed report selection in my harness (not in the branch). Not usable yet. One wrong value would have been produced (DUK).**
1. `find_report_files` picks the wrong page in 25 of 25 cases. Its regex pairs each ShortName with the NEXT report's HtmlFileName (in FilingSummary.xml, HtmlFileName comes before ShortName). The result is always R(n+1), usually the Parenthetical page. Parse per `<Report>` block instead. Also exclude the misspelling "Paranthetical" (BSX 0000885725-22-000006).
2. `parse_number` returns None for pre-2016 cells that carry the element name, e.g. "2,341,284us-gaap_Liabilities" (JPM 0000019617-15-000318; HRL 0001104659-15-017330; VMI 0001047469-15-004183). Strip `[a-z-]+_\w+` first.
3. Footnote cells such as "[1]" can be separate td cells (JPM: '2,341,284', '[1]', '2,341,046', '[1]'). Aligning from the right then reads the footnote as the value. Drop footnote-only cells before aligning.
4. BBIO (0001743881-25-000012) parses with `columns=[]`: the header dates are not recognised, so every cell fails with KeyError.
5. `_TOTAL_LE` misses these L&E labels: "Total liabilities, redeemable noncontrolling interests, and shareholders' equity" (CAH), "TOTAL LIABILITIES, NONCONTROLLING INTERESTS IN THE OPERATING PARTNERSHIP AND SHAREHOLDERS' EQUITY" (RYN), "Total liabilities, preferred units, and equity" (EPD), "TOTAL CAPITALIZATION AND LIABILITIES" (AWK), "Liabilities and Shareholders' equity, Total" (TYL), and a bare "Total" (IDACORP 2023/2020).
6. `_TOTAL_EQ` misses equity lines without "Total": XEL's equity sits on "Common stock — ... shares" 12,222 inside Capitalization (0000072903-19-000010). DUK's NCI-inclusive line is the standard label "Stockholders' Equity, Including Portion Attributable to Noncontrolling Interest".
7. **WRONG VALUE: combined filings.** DUK 2016-03-31 (0001326160-16-000296) stacks 7 registrant blocks. The kit takes the FIRST L&E (121,709) and the LAST total-equity line (a subsidiary's 13,087), giving 108,622 against the face's 81,768. DUK 2020 gives 146,147 against 113,204. Fix: restrict the search to the first registrant block, i.e. the rows up to the first L&E total.
8. AWK hazard: `_MEZZ` would subtract "Redeemable preferred stock at redemption value" (8), but on AWK's face that line sits inside "Total long-term debt". It is debt, not mezzanine: 14,089 would be shown against the right 14,097 if the L&E label were matched.
9. Worked: right-alignment of a leading blank column (XEL), scale detection for both "$ in Millions" and old "(USD $)In Thousands" headers, KHC redeemable preferred 8,320 as mezzanine (20,761), DTE x2, MHK/LAMR printed totals, HRL 2025 "Shareholders' Investment", BSX, LDOS, RYN 2017. `cell_matches` and `seeded_sample` look fine. Not tested: WMT "Nonredeemable noncontrolling interest", where `_MEZZ` matches "redeemable" inside "nonredeemable".
10. Hidden "- Definition ..." popup rows are parsed as data rows. Harmless today, but they should be skipped.

**Will I use the kit for all later verifications?** Yes for sampling and verdict skeletons now. For face reading: yes only after fixes 1-3 and 7 land and this 25-cell set re-runs clean. Until then I use the kit's `parse_rpage` and `face_liabilities` behind my own per-block report selection, and I hand-read every cell where the kit is unavailable or disagrees. Its output alone is not FILING-CHECKED evidence.
