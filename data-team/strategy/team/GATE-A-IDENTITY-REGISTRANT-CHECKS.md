### 3e. Shared-accession primary-filer rule (candidate; not adopted; Data Engineer lane 1 owns the build)
**Rule P1.** For any accession whose submission header lists 2 or more FILER blocks, the primary filer is the CIK in the instance's **default-context (no dimension) `dei:EntityCentralIndexKey`**. Corroborate with the **first FILER block of the submission header** and, where available, FSDS `sub.txt` `cik` for that `adsh`. If they disagree, quarantine the accession (no facts loaded) and raise a finding. Never use: the accession prefix (agent-filed: Eversource 0001628280; filer-account reuse: WEC via 0000107815), companyfacts attribution (SEC moved 4 groups' Q2 2026 10-Qs to subsidiaries), `dei:LegalEntityAxis` in companyfacts (stripped), or CIK sort order (today's behaviour).
**Consequences for load (proposal, not policy):** (1) `core.filing` gets one row per accession owned by the primary filer, plus a link of the other registrant CIKs (a link table, so a subsidiary's own submissions no longer claim the accession); (2) undimensioned facts on a shared accession belong only to the primary filer, whichever CIK's companyfacts served them (EAI's 467 facts move to, or are reloaded for, id 694); (3) non-primary registrants get no values from companyfacts for that accession; their own figures exist only as LegalEntityAxis contexts in the instance and stay excluded unless loaded from the instance (S2 already proposed excluding EAI from the cohort; founder decision 8 in doc 49); (4) a subsidiary row whose only "ticker" is a bond or preferred (EAI, CMS-PB, GPJA) must not get a market cap.
