## Predecessor history (fixed today)

The lane readers (`fsn_company*.py`) read only filings whose `sub.cik` is the company itself. So a predecessor's filings, which are in the directory, were skipped. That hit MDT (Medtronic Inc), LIN (Praxair), AVGO (Avago, Broadcom Ltd), MRVL (Marvell Technology Group), XOM (Exxon Mobil Corp), DIS (Walt Disney Co old) and BLK (BlackRock old).

- `fsn_company.own_ciks` now adds the predecessors named in `FSN_PREDECESSORS` (`pred=succ,...`, the `company_history_source` aliases) and reports their filings under the company's CIK. Other filers stay out.
- `bs_derive_measure.py` passes the same map to `load_company`.
- These seven now have records from 2014. DIS has 41 total-liabilities cells, back to 2013-09-30.
- Tested in `test_fsn_company_predecessors.py`, mutation-checked.
