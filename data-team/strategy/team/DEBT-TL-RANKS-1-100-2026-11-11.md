## Total liabilities (PR #6 derivation, with today's ruling)

`total_liabilities_rows.jsonl.gz` holds CELL-FORMAT rows from `fsn_store.bs_derive`:

| Kind | Cells |
|---|---|
| Calc route | 1,244 |
| Identity route | 7 |
| `not_presented` | 18 |

These come from 27 companies. The other 65 print their own total-liabilities line, which select serves.

**Ruling (Dhairya 2026-11-11):** a sum of printed face lines is allowed only where the calc tree or the section confirms it is complete; otherwise the cell is `not_presented`. The calc route already publishes only when its children tie to the printed liabilities-and-equity total. The identity route is not a sum of lines. A column neither route confirms now gets `not_presented` with reason `total_liabilities_not_printed`, and the stopping reason goes in `input_refs.not_derived`: TMO 5, GILD 6, MCD 5, ETN 2. This is built in `bs_derive.tl_cell`, with a mutation-checked test.

## Predecessor history (fixed today)

The lane readers (`fsn_company*.py`) read only filings whose `sub.cik` is the company itself. So a predecessor's filings, which are in the directory, were skipped. That hit MDT (Medtronic Inc), LIN (Praxair), AVGO (Avago, Broadcom Ltd), MRVL (Marvell Technology Group), XOM (Exxon Mobil Corp), DIS (Walt Disney Co old) and BLK (BlackRock old).

- `fsn_company.own_ciks` now adds the predecessors named in `FSN_PREDECESSORS` (`pred=succ,...`, the `company_history_source` aliases) and reports their filings under the company's CIK. Other filers stay out.
- `bs_derive_measure.py` passes the same map to `load_company`.
- These seven now have records from 2014. DIS has 41 total-liabilities cells, back to 2013-09-30.
- Tested in `test_fsn_company_predecessors.py`, mutation-checked.
