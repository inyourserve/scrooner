Dhairya under delegation: (1) revenue = operating revenue, top line excluding other income, equity-affiliate income and gains (CVX/XOM 'Sales and other operating revenues', not 'Total revenues and other income'; COP 2015-19 aligned). (2) cost_of_revenue = the filer's whole cost node, goods + services (RTX, TMO, VZ, TMUS). (3) Opex for filers without a COR line: printed total is opex (BKNG ruling) only if no band line is cost-of-sales by nature (CostOf* tag, 'cost of' label, food and paper); else lines after the cost-of-sales lines. (4) stockholders_equity is parent equity excl NCI (VZ 2025). (5) net income = parent node, not ProfitLoss (AVGO FY2018). Structural resolver db79aa1c: 99.62% agreement with served PROVEN.
