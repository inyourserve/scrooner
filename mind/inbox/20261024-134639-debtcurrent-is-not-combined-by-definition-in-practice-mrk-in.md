MU 'Current debt' (DebtCurrent 155 = FinanceLeaseLiabilityCurrent 155, 0000723125-21-000065) and GOOG 2015-16 'Short-term debt' include leases, but MRK (0001628280-24-006850 R78), INTC, CSCO (0000858877-25-000111 R82) and UNH file no finance-lease fact at the date and locate operating leases in other liabilities. Label DebtCurrent combined only with same-instant lease evidence.
