OMC tags its face 'Work in process' InventoryWorkInProcess in 2015-2019, but its pre-606 policy note defines it as costs incurred on behalf of clients plus unbilled fees (billed within ~30 days). Exclude from inventory in all eras; quick ratio = current ratio for OMC. Strong footing does not prove inventory membership.
