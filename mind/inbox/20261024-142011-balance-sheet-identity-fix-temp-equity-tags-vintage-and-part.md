Deriving liabilities as Assets - total equity - temporary equity fails three ways: (1) the redeemable NCI / mezzanine line is filed on several elements (TemporaryEquityCarryingAmountIncludingPortionAttributableToNoncontrollingInterests - note plural - , RedeemableNoncontrollingInterestEquityCommonCarryingAmount, ...FairValue, ...OtherCarryingAmount), not only RedeemableNoncontrollingInterestEquityCarryingAmount; (2) terms must come from one filing vintage (IRM mixed original assets with restated equity); (3) parent equity via Assets - Liabilities - RNCI is total equity at partnerships, whose NCI is on PartnersCapitalAttributableToNoncontrollingInterest. When no total liabilities line is printed, the check is the sum of face liability lines with mezzanine kept outside.
