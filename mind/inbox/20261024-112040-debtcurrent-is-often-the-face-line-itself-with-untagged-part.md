MRK 'Loans payable and current portion of LTD' (us-gaap_DebtCurrent, 0000310158-15-000005 R4), INTC/CSCO 'Short-term debt', UNH 'Short-term borrowings and current maturities' are face lines tagged DebtCurrent with only commercial paper (or nothing) tagged as a part. A parts-reconciliation test falsely holds correct totals; accept face-presented DebtCurrent. ABBV shows a separate ShortTermBorrowings face line beside lease-inclusive debt lines; CVX has a separate noncurrent capital-lease face line.
