Dhairya 2026-11-13: (a) bank operating_expenses = NoninterestExpense, mapped. (b) insurer totals incl. benefits/losses = combined; component_sum only if printed non-benefit expense lines tie. (c) shares_outstanding at multi-class filers = sum of class members at BS date (fsn_xbrl). (d) note-only InterestExpenseDebt: mapped (stmt NOTE) if it is the gross charge and no broader interest total exists in the filing; else component_in_note. (e) dividend paid to former parent pre-spin is not dividends_paid (not_presented, pre_spin_parent_distribution). (f) finance leases stated immaterial with no amount: total_debt served without leases, note finance_lease_immaterial_per_filing; hold only for material undisclosed totals (FCX). (g) MLP weighted basic/diluted units = weighted shares. Opex key = component_sum when the printed band between GP and OI ties (not combined).
