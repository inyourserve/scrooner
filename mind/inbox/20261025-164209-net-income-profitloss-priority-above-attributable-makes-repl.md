net_income maps NetIncomeLoss(1), ProfitLoss(2), NetIncomeLossAvailableToCommonStockholdersBasic(3). For filers without NetIncomeLoss, ProfitLoss (consolidated, incl. NCI) wins: FCX 5,365M vs 4,306M attributable, CAT, TXRH, PCG, THC; 118 cohort retirement replacements at 19 companies. Prefer the attributable tag over ProfitLoss whenever both are filed.
