Borrowing one cal per company failed for filers whose face structure changed (TMUS, RYN, ALB, EPD: 109 of batch-2 instants failed the identity under a borrowed graph); fetching per failing era cost 68 requests for 10 companies, which does not fit the 2,600 cap. What works: reading B = a us-gaap TOTAL element (LiabilitiesCurrent, LiabilitiesNoncurrent, LiabilitiesOtherThanLongtermDebtNoncurrent) covers the liability lines printed before it since the previous total; it is an element rule, not a label rule, offered as an ALTERNATIVE reading beside the cal roots (reading A), and the balance-sheet identity on the same accession chooses; capitalization-format filers (EMR, FE, NI, AWK print debt before current liabilities) tie on A, TMUS/ALB (filer cal omits the total-to-components arc) tie on B. Zero-cal trial over the 271 remaining face-sum companies: 5,356 of 5,653 cells tie; 130 eras at 43 companies + 19 no-structure eras still need their own cal (~149 files). Every record stores which reading won; the Verifier samples reading-B instants explicitly (coincidental-tie risk). Debt is being re-measured the same way with the filer's note carrying totals as the gate.
