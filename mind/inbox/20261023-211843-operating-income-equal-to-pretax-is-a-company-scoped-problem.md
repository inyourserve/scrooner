Stored-vs-stored scan of all 986 cohort companies: 58 matches within 0.1% at 48 companies in the new pass, all isolated 1-3 periods (coincidence), persistent only at BRO (6 periods, derived OpEx+Revenues path), SF (2 FY), ELS (1 quarter; pretax stored for only 2 of 15 periods) and AFG (FY2025 1,073M = raw domestic 1,031M + foreign 42M pretax tags). About 119 company-batches had no stored pretax and are untestable this way. MOG-A and MET are different mechanisms (not on the face). Use company-scoped holds, not a population rule.
