Dhairya 2026-11-13 (HD Goodwill 2015-02-01: FY2018 10-K note copy 2,102M overrode 7 face copies of 1,353M; 9,959 such facts across 105 of top-110). A copy is authoritative-eligible only if its filing prints that period as a face column: instants period_of_report-end<=400d; durations 10-K <=2y+10d, 10-Q <=400d; original filing always eligible; latest eligible wins, ties lower accession. Same eligibility function used by proof R13.
