From the 2026-11-21 filing-agent reads: (a) sbc = cash-flow 'Stock-based compensation' add-back whenever printed, never the note expense measure (BAC 2015 = -89M, not 2,170M). (b) cash_paid_for_interest = InterestPaidNet wherever printed at its latest print (XOM FY2017/18 383/303); where only the total incl. capitalized interest is printed, serve it flagged includes_capitalized. (c) The latest-print rule never replaces a value with a rounded copy of the same figure (XOM sbc FY2024 835M stays, not 0.8B). (d) Any non-interest line in the operating-income-to-pretax band is other income (BLK net gain on investments; LIN 2016 net pension/OPEB non-service cost -9M); extends ruling 5 of 2026-11-17. (e) 'Combined' is judged by the printed label, not the element name (BLK D&A tagged DepreciationAmortizationAndAccretionNet, printed 'Depreciation and amortization', serves as D&A). (f) A successor registrant's company includes its predecessor CIK's 10-Ks (BLK 0001364742 before 0002012383).
