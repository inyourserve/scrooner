Stored-vs-stored cohort scan (2026-10-23, partial): AFG has no stored pretax concept, but its FY2025 operating income 1,073M equals raw domestic pretax 1,031M plus foreign pretax 42M (two raw tags), so the displayed operating income is really income before tax. MET (6,137, not on the face) and MOG-A are a different mechanism (segment-note figure), so they stay single-tag company-scoped holds. BRO, SF and ELS also match pretax within 0.1% in some periods.
