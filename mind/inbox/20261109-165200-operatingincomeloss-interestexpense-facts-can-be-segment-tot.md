Non-dimensional annual OperatingIncomeLoss was ELV "Reportable segments' operating gain" (reconciles to pretax), SLB "Pretax segment operating income", IP segment operating profit, and WPC income before taxes in the tax note. DAL InterestExpense was the NET line. A template IN/OUT or note-sourced value decided on tag presence alone was wrong in 6 of 15 rows.
