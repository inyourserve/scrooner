In FSN num a 10-K carries qtrs=1 facts at the fiscal-year-end ddate for NetIncomeLoss, revenue, GrossProfit and EarningsPerShareDiluted when it has a quarterly financial data note. MSFT does so FY2011-FY2020 (FY2019 Q4 diluted EPS 1.71 in 0001564590-19-027952) and stops from the FY2021 10-K 0001564590-21-039151 (S-K Item 302(a) relief, 2021). The FSN pilot (run 41490670432) marks MSFT Q4 EPS FY2014-FY2020 not_filed_separately and derives Q4 NI by YTD although the print exists (values equal). Rule: a qtrs=1 print at the FY end in the 10-K wins over derivation (note-sourced stratum); per-share from it is allowed because it is printed.
