PPL 2022-06 DPS: 0.225 in both 2022 10-Qs, 0.24 (the 2023 rate) in three 2023 10-Qs; RSG, PPL 2017-11, OC 2019, NSC, OGE show the same pattern with one later copy. Dividends are not restated, so: agreeing own-vintage copies (filed within 180 days) win; otherwise the original stands unless >= 2 later copies agree (TEX, DCI, OC 2020, COO, AMGN 2025, TSCO originals were the wrong ones). EPS differs: three vintages (Dominion Q1 2024 0.78/0.50/0.46) are real restatements and are held. Also: a stock-dividend value tag alone is not split evidence (EQIX, IRM REIT special distributions); a per-share move with no share-count move is a restatement (IONS).
