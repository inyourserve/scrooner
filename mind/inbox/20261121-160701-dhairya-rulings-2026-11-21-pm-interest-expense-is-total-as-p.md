(1) interest_expense = total interest expense as printed on the income statement or stated in MD&A, incl. finance-lease and financing-obligation interest (matches the total_debt perimeter); CRM FY2018/19 86.9M/154M stand. (2) stockholders_equity: serve the Schedule I parent-company line when it ties exactly to total equity minus nonredeemable NCI (UNH); otherwise derive total equity minus NCI from ONE filing, flagged derived (replaces the PG hide precedent; T, VZ, PG). (3) sbc disclosed only after tax is never served as sbc; hide as only_after_tax_disclosed with the quote (VZ FY2017-25, GE FY2015-17). (4) One sbc source per row: if no cash-flow add-back is printed, every year uses the equity-statement line (CB). (5) No gross profit when revenue and cost of sales cover different perimeters (GE insurance revenue). (6) No derivation with inputs from two filings, even with a stated change (T interest_income 2017).
