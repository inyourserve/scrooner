META's 2019 finance-lease noncurrent 418 is printed in the Dec 31 2018 column (473 = 55 + 418; 2018 financing cash flows from finance leases = 0). AMZN 2016 CapitalLeaseObligations = gross 9,406 (PV 9,077). Use the PV row or current + long-term; require a face column or cash-flow witness for a note fact at an instant.
