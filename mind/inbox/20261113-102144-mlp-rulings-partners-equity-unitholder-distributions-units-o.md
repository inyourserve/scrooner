Dhairya 2026-11-13 (EPD, ranks 151-160): for MLPs stockholders_equity = total partners' equity attributable to the partnership (excl. NCI); dividends_paid = distributions paid to common unitholders (excl. NCI distributions; preferred units combined, like preferred dividends); shares_outstanding = common units outstanding at BS date (class sum, never dei, never issued minus treasury). Extends the REIT distributions-paid ruling.
