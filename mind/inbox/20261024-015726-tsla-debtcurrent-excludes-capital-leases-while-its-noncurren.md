TSLA 2017 10-Qs: DebtCurrent (debt note, e.g. 760,284K at 2017-06-30) is debt only incl. related-party notes; LongTermDebtAndCapitalLeaseObligations (7,122,862K) includes capital leases; a noncurrent 'Convertible senior notes issued to related parties' line is separate. Summing DebtCurrent + the lease-inclusive line gave 7,883,146K vs face 7,941,839K (0001564590-17-015705 R2). Never pair lines of different lease perimeters.
