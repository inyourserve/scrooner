The as-filed pilot's limits (ddate month-rounded, no start date, no calc linkbase) are FSDS limits only. FSN tables: SUB, TAG, DIM (dimh=MD5 of axis=member pairs), NUM (all values as rendered incl. notes; datp = date offset from rounded ddate, durp = duration offset, dcml = decimals), TXT (all non-numeric facts, incl. dei flags and text blocks), REN, PRE, CAL (grp, arc, negative weight). Published monthly about 1 week after month end.
