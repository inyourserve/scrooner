Dhairya ruling under founder delegation, answering the Tag Expert on resolver .76. EPS, DPS, weighted shares and shares outstanding take the latest print, i.e. split-adjusted history, so series stay comparable. The own-print value is kept as evidence, and the cell is flagged split_adjusted when it differs by a split ratio. Taking a printed restated EPS is not derivation; EPS stays never-derived. total_debt follows one vintage too.
