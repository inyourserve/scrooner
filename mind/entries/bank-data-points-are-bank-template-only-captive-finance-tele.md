Dhairya 2026-11-16: provision_for_credit_losses, loans_net, deposits, net_interest_income, noninterest income/expense, total_interest_income are served only for bank-template companies (bank income statement). For all others (captive-finance CAT/DE/GE, VZ device receivables, CSCO financing receivables, WELL REIT loans, CB insurer) they are OUT, never served. Reason: comparability; captive finance is a segment, not a bank. Library-learned labels caught 55 false net_interest_income values at industrials ('Interest, net'). MO/PM excise in COR stays (ruling A).
