Replaces ruling 10 of 2026-11-17 for note data points (cash_paid_for_interest, amortization_of_intangibles): when a company stops disclosing a figure (GOOG interest paid after FY2018, GOOG amortization FY2023-24, NVDA interest paid FY2026), the year hides as structural_not_presented ONLY if a full read or full-text search of every later 10-K and 10-Q finds no amount, and the quote names that search. A partial-period figure (9M only) stays open until the 10-K comparative prints. Also 2026-11-22: multi-class diluted shares = Class A diluted (incl. B conversion) + Class C diluted, never adding B; pre-split class sums x split ratio are allowed derived share counts (EPS never derived); a text-stated 'never declared or paid' dividend is VALUE 0.
