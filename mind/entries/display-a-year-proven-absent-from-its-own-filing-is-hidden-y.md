Dhairya 2026-11-16 (706 cells, e.g. QCOM interest paid FY2016-25): when the resolver proves absence in that year's own 10-K (no face line AND no undimensioned fact; decision not_presented, never inferred), the cell is HIDDEN_YEAR with the accession as evidence, flag absent_in_own_filing. Never hide a year a later filing prints (fill/replay instead). Buybacks/dividends with no line are hidden, not 0 (0 needs a printed dash). Ritwik samples these hides.
