Of 472K company-tag pairs, 391K are standard tags (3,852 tags, 24% decided on 2026-11-11) and 81K custom (10,008 tags, 14% decided). 95 standard tags cover 50% of pairs, 359 cover 80%, 744 cover 90%, 1,250 cover 95%. Basics were almost wholly undecided: financing CF 0/731 companies, Goodwill 7/593, PP&E net 5/569. Fastest wins: abstract/axis/domain/member tags excluded as presentation-only; the top-95 standard tags mapped on their home statement; custom tags by cal-tree structure.
