Companies that print no operating income line on the IS often still file a non-dimensional us-gaap:OperatingIncomeLoss in the segment note: it is the SUM of segment profit before corporate costs, interest and other items. MOG-A 0001628280-25-054103 'Total operating profit' 449,606 = sum of 4 segments; SHW 0000089800-24-000033 'Segment profit' 4,161.7; SWK 0000093556-22-000004 'Segment Profit' 2,176.0 (later years move it under OperatingSegmentsMember). EHC 'Operating earnings' is parent-attributable (excludes NCI). Rule (approver 2026-11-03): never take operating income from outside the IS face; such companies are template-OUT for operating income. Contrast: gross interest expense from the other income/expense note IS reliable (9/9 PASS: INTC, MRK, IP, BIIB, G, DIS, GOOG, PYPL, NUE); DIS stores it with sign '-' (-1,812M), correct +1,812M.
