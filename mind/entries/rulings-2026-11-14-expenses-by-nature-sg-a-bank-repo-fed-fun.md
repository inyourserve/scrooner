Dhairya: (a) no OI line (DIS): restructuring/impairment are operating; OI not_presented; other-income band = non-interest lines after the last operating line to pretax. (b) dividends_paid never includes preferred; a line including preferred is combined. (c) IS by nature (BKNG personnel/marketing/G&A): SG&A not_presented, reason expenses_by_nature. (d) Fed funds purchased and repos are excluded from total_debt; a line mixing them with borrowings is combined (COF). (e) D&A: the purest line wins (UBER IS 711 over CF 737). (f) Filer XBRL error confirmed from the printed HTML (Marvell 2016 R6, T 2019 equity income, GE 2016 taxes paid): serve the printed value with evidence.
