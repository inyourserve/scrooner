Founder rule relayed 2026-11-01: in the company-wise FSN statement store no template cell may be silently empty. Each cell of a sector's statement template is either a proven value or a blank with a named reason (e.g. line_presented_combined_with_others, mixed_vintage_inputs, no_basis_witness, filing_newer_than_fsn_set). DOC-CITED: the reason names come from the design doc; the exact rule wording is the founder's and should be re-read in the design before coding.
