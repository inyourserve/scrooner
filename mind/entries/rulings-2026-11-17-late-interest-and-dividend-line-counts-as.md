Dhairya rulings under delegation. (1) A combined 'interest and dividends income' line counts as interest income for the OI-to-pretax band, so other_income_expense_net = the remaining non-interest lines (MSFT and every such filer); this refines the 'split interest where the note gives it' rule. (2) interest_income is the consolidated company total; a figure excluding a segment (DE 'excluding FS') is a part and is never served. (3) When a CF cash definition changes across years (C 2018-19), each year takes its latest print, flagged definition_change.
